# Full Script

One of the most memorable experiences I had at this park happened last fall.

There was a small weekend festival at the park, so I decided to go there alone to relax and enjoy the atmosphere.

There were several food trucks, small booths, and live music performances throughout the park.

A lot of people were walking around, eating delicious food, and taking pictures.

The atmosphere was lively, and I was having a great time.

While I was walking around, I decided to visit a bench that I used to sit on with my ex-girlfriend.

When we were dating, we often came to this park together and usually sat on the same bench.

However, as I walked toward it, I suddenly saw my ex-girlfriend sitting there with another guy.

I really didn't expect to see her, so I was completely shocked.

To make things worse, I was wearing very casual clothes because I hadn't planned to meet anyone.

I felt extremely embarrassed and awkward.

So I pretended not to see them and quickly walked away.

At the time, the situation was very uncomfortable.

However, when I look back on it now, I actually think it was pretty funny.

That's why it has become one of my most unforgettable memories at the park.
