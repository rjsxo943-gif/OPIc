# Full Script

There are quite a few pretty parks in my city.

Like I said in the previous question, an especially nice one is only about fifteen minutes from where I live.

The park in my neighborhood is called North Port Waterfront Park, and I really like going there to spend some time in nature.

So I go to the park at least a couple of times a week.

I usually go alone because I like to exercise at the crack of dawn.

I wouldn't mind going with friends, but nobody wants a wake-up call at 5:30 in the morning.

However, when I just want to take a walk during the spring, I usually go with my friends or family.

The main activity I do at the park is running.

The park is perfect for running because it has a nice running path.

My usual routine is to run about three kilometers, which takes around fifteen minutes.

If I still have enough energy, I use the outdoor workout machines.

After that, I stretch my arms and legs.

Finally, I cool down and usually grab an energy drink.

I love working out at the park because I always feel healthy and refreshed afterwards.

Personally, I'd much rather be out in nature than run on a treadmill.

All in all, I think going to the park is one of the best ways to stay healthy.
