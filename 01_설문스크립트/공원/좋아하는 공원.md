# Full Script

I really like going to parks, so I've been to a lot of beautiful parks in my country.

Actually, I live in a big city, Busan, but there are several nice parks nearby.

So I go to the park at least once or twice a week, and often a lot more during the spring.

Speaking of the park I like to go to, there is a park called North Port Waterfront Park right behind Busan Station.

Fortunately, it's located within walking distance of my home.

It takes about fifteen minutes to get there on foot, so it's very convenient and easy for me to visit regularly.

In addition, what makes North Port Waterfront Park special is that it has some great facilities.

There are walking trails all over the park, and there are plenty of benches where people can sit down and relax.

On top of that, the park has everything you would expect, like soccer fields, basketball courts, and beautiful gardens.

The gardens are my favorite part of the park because the flowers begin to blossom every spring.

I mean, there are so many flowers and plants, and they give off an incredible scent.

So whenever I go to North Port Waterfront Park, it always feels like I'm out in the countryside even though I'm right in the middle of a big city.

Overall, I feel very lucky to have such a beautiful park close to my home.

I would say North Port Waterfront Park is an excellent place to hang out with friends or family.

Although the park gets quite crowded sometimes, it's a perfect place to enjoy nature.
