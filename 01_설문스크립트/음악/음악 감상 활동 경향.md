## Full Script

I absolutely love listening to music, and I would say that it has become a big part of my daily routine.

My taste in music is fairly broad, but these days, I mostly listen to Korean hip-hop and classical music. The kind of music I choose usually depends on what I am doing at the time.

I listen to music most often when I am working out. When I exercise, I usually put on rap music, especially energetic Korean hip-hop songs. The strong beats give me energy and help me stay focused on each movement. They also help me keep going when I start to feel tired, so music is almost essential whenever I work out.

On the other hand, when I am studying or reading a book, I prefer listening to classical music without any lyrics. Songs with lyrics can sometimes distract me because I end up paying attention to the words. Classical music is much calmer, so it helps me clear my mind and concentrate for a longer period of time.

Besides working out, studying, and reading, I sometimes listen to music while taking a walk. However, I would say that I listen to it the most at the gym.

As for the device I use, I usually listen to music on my smartphone with my AirPods. They are convenient because I can carry them around and listen to music wherever I am.

I mostly use an app called Melon. I have created several playlists on it, so I can quickly choose one depending on my mood or what I am doing. I also use playlists on YouTube, especially when I want to listen to a long classical music playlist while studying or reading. I like using playlists because I do not have to stop what I am doing and choose a new song every few minutes.

What I like most about music is that it helps me concentrate in different ways. Rap music gives me energy and helps me focus on my workout, while classical music calms my mind and helps me focus on studying or reading.

So, for me, music is not just background noise. It is something that helps me control my mood and stay focused. Honestly, I cannot imagine working out or studying without it.

## IH Points

### 1. 음악을 하나의 장르로만 설명하지 않음

운동할 때는 랩을 듣고, 공부나 독서를 할 때는 클래식을 듣는다고 구분하여 답변에 구체성을 더했다.

### 2. 단순한 취향이 아니라 이유까지 설명함

랩은 운동 에너지와 동작 집중에 도움을 주고, 클래식은 가사가 없어 공부와 독서에 집중하기 좋다는 이유를 설명했다.

### 3. 기기와 앱을 구체적으로 언급함

스마트폰, 에어팟, 멜론, 유튜브 플레이리스트를 자연스럽게 연결했다.

### 4. 개인적인 의미로 마무리함

음악을 단순한 배경음악이 아니라 기분과 집중 상태를 조절하는 도구라고 표현했다.
