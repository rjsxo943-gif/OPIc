## Full Script

Hi Eva, nice to meet you.

I'm doing pretty well, but honestly, I'm a little nervous right now.
Still, I'll try my best to answer your questions.

My name is GeonTae Kim.
I don't have an English name, so you can just call me GeonTae.

I'm 25 years old.

currently a senior majoring in electronic engineering, and I'm expected to graduate soon.

So these days, I'm preparing to get a job as an engineer.

To be honest, I first chose electronic engineering because I thought it would give me good job opportunities, especially at big companies.

But after studying it for several years, I realized that this field actually suits me pretty well.

These days, I'm interested in smart factories and automation systems.

Recently, I worked on my graduation project.

It was about detecting drone signals using SDR.

Through that project, I learned how to collect signal data, analyze it, and build a real-time detection system.

It was challenging, but I felt proud because I could connect what I learned in school with a real engineering problem.

Also, I'm currently teaching math to a student who is preparing for the GED.

Teaching math requires a lot of patience because I have to explain things step by step depending on the student's level.

But I think this experience has helped me become more responsible and patient.

As for my personality, I like spending time alone, but I also enjoy meeting people.

My close friends usually say I'm funny and responsible.

I think that's pretty accurate.

When I have free time, I like working out, traveling, and visiting good restaurants.

Working out helps me relieve stress, and I like the feeling of focusing on myself.

When I stay at home, I usually watch YouTube, read books, or talk with my family.

In the future, I want to become a capable engineer who is recognized by others.

At the same time, I also want to be a warm-hearted son, husband, and father.

So I guess that's everything about me.

I'm really looking forward to starting a new chapter in my life.
