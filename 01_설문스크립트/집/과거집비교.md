# 질문

- How is your current home different from your previous home?
- Tell me how your current home is different from your old home.
- Compare your current home with the place where you used to live.

# Full Script

When I was young, I lived with my family in a spacious apartment.

It was the place where I grew up and spent my teenage years.

But I moved out of my parents' home about three years ago.

So let me compare the home I lived in before with my current home.

In the past, I lived in a large apartment with my family.

It had several bedrooms, two bathrooms, and a large living room.

There was also a kitchen and a dining area, so my family often spent time together there.

Also, the living room had big windows, so it was very bright during the day.

In contrast, my current home is much smaller.

Now, I live in a small studio apartment by myself.

Unlike my parents' home, everything is in one open space, like my bed, desk, small kitchen area, and living area.

So I don't have a lot of furniture.

I only keep the things I really need, like my bed, desk, and some kitchen appliances.

But on the plus side, my current apartment is very cozy and quiet.

Also, I really like having my own space.

I can study, relax, watch YouTube, or read books without being disturbed.

Overall, I'm quite satisfied with where I live now.

It is small, but it is comfortable, and for me, it feels like a safe haven.
