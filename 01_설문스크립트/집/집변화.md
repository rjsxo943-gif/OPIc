# Full Script

I haven't made huge changes to my home because my studio apartment is not that big.

But I have made some small changes to make it more comfortable.

The biggest change was around my bed.

A while ago, I bought a new bed because my old bed was not comfortable.

After changing my bed, I started sleeping much better, so I think it was a really good decision.

I also bought a thick beige rug and put it on the floor.

It made my apartment feel much warmer and cozier.

Another small change was that I rearranged some furniture.

I moved my bed and desk a little closer to the window.

Now, I can sit there, drink coffee, watch YouTube, or read books while getting some sunlight.

Overall, these were not huge changes, but they made my home much better.

Now my apartment feels more like my own place.

It is small, but it is cozy, comfortable, and it has become my safe haven.
