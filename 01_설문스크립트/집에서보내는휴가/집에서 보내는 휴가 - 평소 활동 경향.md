# 집에서 보내는 휴가 - 평소 활동 경향

## Question

- How do you spend your vacations at home?
- What do you usually do?
- Who do you spend time with?
- Give me as many details as possible.

## Full Script

I actually enjoy spending some of my vacations at home.

I like traveling and going out with friends, but when I feel physically or mentally tired, staying at home is the best way for me to recover. Since I usually live alone, I can spend the entire day exactly the way I want without worrying about anyone else.

On a typical day off, I wake up at around ten in the morning. The first thing I do is drink a large glass of water, probably about four hundred milliliters. Then I wash my face and have a simple breakfast.

After that, I usually lie down and watch YouTube or read webtoons. I especially enjoy travel channels like Pani Bottle and KwakTube. Watching their videos allows me to experience different countries without actually leaving my room.

When I have a book that I am interested in, I also spend some time reading. I normally read on my bed or couch while listening to quiet music. Unlike regular weekdays, I do not force myself to exercise or do anything productive. I prefer to completely relax and give my body a break.

When I am alone, I usually eat whatever I already have at home. However, when I stay at my parents' place and my family is around, we sometimes order something delicious, usually chicken or pizza, and eat it together.

If I start to feel bored or uncomfortable after staying indoors for too long, I go out for a short walk. Sometimes, I take a book to a nearby café and read there for a while. That gives me a change of scenery without making the day too busy.

What I like most about a stay-at-home vacation is that I do not have to spend much money. I can stay in a cool and comfortable home, enjoy some time alone, and catch up on all the YouTube videos and webtoons I have been saving.

For me, a good vacation does not always have to involve traveling somewhere. Sometimes, staying home and doing almost nothing is exactly what I need to recharge.

## IH Points

### 1. 하루의 흐름이 자연스러움

기상부터 아침 식사, 콘텐츠 감상, 식사, 산책까지 시간 순서로 설명했다.

### 2. 혼자 있을 때와 가족과 있을 때를 구분함

혼자 있을 때는 집에 있는 음식을 먹고, 가족과 있을 때는 치킨이나 피자를 주문한다고 설명했다.

### 3. 실제로 즐겨 보는 콘텐츠가 포함됨

빠니보틀과 곽튜브를 언급해 개인적인 답변으로 만들었다.

### 4. 집에만 머무르지 않는 활동도 포함함

답답할 때는 산책하거나 카페에서 책을 읽는다고 말해 답변이 단조롭지 않다.

### 5. 휴가의 의미로 마무리함

집에서 아무것도 하지 않는 것도 몸과 마음을 회복하는 좋은 휴가라고 정리했다.

## Notes

- 핵심 캐릭터는 `여행도 좋아하지만 피곤할 때는 혼자 집에서 완전히 쉬는 사람`이다.
- `couch potato`는 다소 부정적으로 들릴 수 있어 사용하지 않았다.
- 핵심 흐름은 `아침 루틴 → 유튜브·웹툰·독서 → 음식 → 산책·카페 → 회복`이다.
- 길다면 물의 정확한 양이나 가족과 음식을 주문하는 부분부터 줄이면 된다.
