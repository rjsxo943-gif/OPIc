# 집에서 보내는 휴가 - 휴가 관련 이슈

## Question

- Why do you think people need vacations?
- What purposes do vacations have for different people?
- How do people spend their vacations differently?

## Full Script

To be honest, I think people need vacations for many different reasons.

Most people spend a lot of time working, studying, or dealing with responsibilities. After finishing something stressful, they need some time to clear their minds, recover from their exhaustion, and spend time with family members they have not seen very often.

However, people do not all spend their vacations in the same way.

Some people prefer to fill their schedules with trips, activities, and meetings with friends. They want to visit new places, try new food, and create special memories. For them, staying busy during a vacation makes them feel that they have used their time well.

On the other hand, some people prefer to stay at home and do almost nothing. They may sleep late, watch TV, order good food, or simply enjoy some time alone. I think this can also be a very good vacation, especially when someone is physically or mentally exhausted.

Personally, I enjoy both kinds of vacations. When I want to make new memories, I like traveling or meeting my friends. However, when I am tired and need to recover, I would rather stay at home and relax.

At this point in my life, I think a quiet vacation at home is more important for me. I have been busy with studying, a boot camp, and preparing for my future career. Even if I get a day off, I may spend a little time reviewing what I have learned or checking my progress. Still, I need enough time to rest because when I do not rest properly, I cannot concentrate and my efficiency drops.

So, in my opinion, there is no single perfect way to spend a vacation. If someone needs new experiences, traveling may be the best choice. If someone needs recovery, staying home may be better.

The most important thing is to spend a vacation in a way that matches your purpose. A good vacation should help you return to your daily life feeling refreshed and ready to move forward again.

## IH Points

### 1. 사람들의 휴가 방식을 두 가지로 나눔

여행과 활동을 선호하는 사람과 집에서 회복하기를 원하는 사람을 비교했다.

### 2. 어느 한쪽만 옳다고 주장하지 않음

휴가의 목적이 추억인지 휴식인지에 따라 좋은 방식이 달라진다고 설명했다.

### 3. 현재의 개인 상황을 반영함

공부, 부트캠프, 취업 준비로 인해 지금은 집에서 회복하는 휴가가 더 필요하다고 말했다.

### 4. 휴식과 효율의 관계를 설명함

제대로 쉬지 않으면 집중력과 효율이 떨어진다는 구체적인 이유를 제시했다.

### 5. 명확한 기준으로 마무리함

자신이 원하는 목적에 맞는 휴가가 가장 좋은 휴가라고 정리했다.
