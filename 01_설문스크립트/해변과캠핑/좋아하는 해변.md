# Full Script

That’s a good question because I’m originally from Busan, and I’ve been to many beaches there.

Among them, my favorite beach is Gwangalli Beach.

It’s one of the most popular beaches in Busan, and I’ve been going there since I was young.

Gwangalli Beach is located in Suyeong-gu, Busan.

It’s not very far from my home, so it usually takes me about 30 minutes to get there by bus.

I normally go there with my friends, especially when the weather is nice.

Since it’s easy to get there by public transportation, we can visit the beach without making any special plans.

What I like most about Gwangalli Beach is its beautiful scenery.

It has a long sandy beach, and I really enjoy listening to the sound of the waves.

During the daytime, my friends and I usually spread out a picnic mat on the sand and sit there while talking and eating snacks.

Sometimes, we sit on a bench or take a long walk along the beach.

I enjoy both the lively atmosphere during the day and the peaceful atmosphere later in the evening.

The sunset is beautiful, but the night view is even more impressive because you can see Gwangandaegyo Bridge shining over the water.

All in all, Gwangalli Beach is my favorite place because I can enjoy both a lively city atmosphere and a relaxing beach atmosphere.

Whether I’m walking along the beach or sitting on a picnic mat, I always have a great time there.

Going to Gwangalli Beach is one of the best ways for me to clear my head and relieve stress.
