# Full Script

When I go camping near the beach, I usually go with three of my close friends.

Since we normally stay for one night, we try to bring only the things we really need.

However, we still end up packing quite a lot because we need camping equipment, clothes, food, and drinks.

The most important things we bring are a tent, sleeping bags, camping chairs, and a small table.

We also pack a change of clothes, towels, and comfortable shoes.

Since the campsite is close to the beach, we sometimes bring swimsuits as well.

For cooking, we bring a portable camping stove, a small grill, and some basic cooking tools.

As for food, we usually pack meat, vegetables, instant noodles, snacks, and plenty of drinks.

We also make sure to bring bottled water, sunscreen, insect repellent, and a portable charger.

Oh, and one more thing—we always bring a Bluetooth speaker because we enjoy listening to music while eating and talking.

Now that I think about it, we bring quite a lot of stuff even for a short camping trip.

Still, every item is useful, and sharing the equipment among four people makes it much easier to carry and set up.

So, I would say being well prepared makes our beach camping trips much more comfortable and enjoyable.
