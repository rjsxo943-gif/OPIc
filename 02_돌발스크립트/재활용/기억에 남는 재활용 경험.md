# 재활용 - 기억에 남는 재활용 경험

## Question

- Tell me about a memorable experience you had while recycling.
- What happened?
- How did you deal with the situation?

---

## Speaking Order

택배 상자와 재활용품을 오래 모아둠

↓

한꺼번에 분리수거장으로 옮김

↓

봉투가 찢어져 캔과 페트병이 쏟아짐

↓

이웃의 도움으로 다시 정리함

↓

앞으로 자주 버리기로 결심함

---

## Full Script

One memorable recycling experience happened when I had allowed too many boxes and recyclable items to pile up at home.

I had received several deliveries, so there were cardboard boxes, plastic bottles, and cans everywhere.

I kept telling myself that I would take them out later, but I continued putting it off.

One evening, I finally decided to take everything to the recycling area at once.

I carried a stack of flattened boxes in one hand and a large plastic bag in the other.

Unfortunately, the bag was too heavy, and it suddenly tore on the way downstairs.

Several plastic bottles and cans fell out and rolled across the floor.

I felt embarrassed because another resident was walking by at that moment.

Luckily, the person helped me pick everything up.

We put the bottles and cans back into a new bag, and I made several trips instead of trying to carry everything at once.

It was not a serious problem, but it was annoying and a little embarrassing.

It also took much longer than it would have if I had recycled regularly.

Since then, I have tried to take out recyclable items before they pile up too much.

I learned that putting off a small household task usually creates a much bigger job later.

---

## Key Expressions

- allow something to pile up
- keep putting it off
- all at once
- roll across the floor
- make several trips
