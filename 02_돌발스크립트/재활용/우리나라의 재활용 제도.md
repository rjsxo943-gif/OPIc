## Full Script

Recycling is a big part of daily life in Korea, and I would say that the recycling system here is fairly well organized.

Most apartment buildings and residential areas have a separate recycling area. People usually sort their waste into different categories, such as paper, plastic, cans, glass bottles, and vinyl packaging.

Regular trash and food waste are handled separately as well. For regular trash, people have to use special garbage bags that they purchase from local stores. Food waste is usually placed in a separate container or disposed of in a designated food waste machine.

Before throwing recyclable items away, people are expected to clean them properly. For example, we empty any liquid or food left inside containers, rinse them with water, and let them dry. We also remove labels from plastic bottles and take tape off cardboard boxes.

After that, we place each item in the correct recycling bin. In many apartment buildings, residents can take out their recycling on certain days of the week. In other places, the recycling area may be open every day.

Of course, recycling can sometimes be a little inconvenient. It takes extra time to wash containers and separate everything correctly. Also, the rules can be confusing because different materials sometimes look very similar.

Still, I think most Koreans are used to doing it because we have practiced separating waste for a long time. It has simply become part of our routine.

Personally, I think recycling is important because it reduces the amount of trash that has to be burned or buried. It also allows materials such as plastic, paper, and metal to be used again.

Overall, the system is not perfect, but I believe it is an effective way to protect the environment and save resources.

## IH Points

### 1. 재활용품 종류를 구체적으로 설명함

종이, 플라스틱, 캔, 유리병, 비닐뿐 아니라 일반 쓰레기와 음식물 쓰레기까지 구분했다.

### 2. 실제 분리수거 과정을 단계별로 설명함

내용물을 비우고, 용기를 씻고, 라벨과 테이프를 제거한 뒤 종류별 수거함에 넣는 순서를 제시했다.

### 3. 장점뿐 아니라 불편한 점도 언급함

재활용이 번거롭고 재질을 구분하기 어려울 때도 있다는 현실적인 내용을 넣었다.

### 4. 한국인의 생활 습관과 연결함

오랫동안 분리수거를 해왔기 때문에 재활용이 자연스러운 일상이 되었다고 설명했다.

### 5. 환경적 의미로 마무리함

쓰레기의 양을 줄이고 자원을 다시 사용할 수 있다는 이유로 재활용의 중요성을 정리했다.
