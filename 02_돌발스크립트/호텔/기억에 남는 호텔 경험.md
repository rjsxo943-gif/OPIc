# 호텔 - 기억에 남는 호텔 경험

## Question

- Tell me about a memorable experience you had at a hotel.
- Where was the hotel?
- Who were you with?
- What made the experience memorable?

---

## Speaking Order

홍콩 여행 → 작은 호텔 → 좋은 위치 → 빅토리아 하버 → 다시 가고 싶음

---

## Full Script

One of my most memorable hotel experiences / was in Hong Kong / with a close friend.

We stayed / at a small hotel / near public transportation.

When I first entered the room, / I was surprised / because it was much smaller / than I expected.

Still, / the room was clean, / the bed was comfortable, / and the location was great.

We could easily visit / Tsim Sha Tsui / and Victoria Harbour.

During the day, / we walked around the city / and tried local food.

At night, / we went to Victoria Harbour / and enjoyed the city lights.

After that, / we went back to the hotel, / ate some snacks, / and talked about our day.

The hotel was not luxurious, / but the location made our trip / very convenient.

That is why / I still remember the hotel, / and I would love to visit / Hong Kong again.

---

## 직독직해

내 가장 기억에 남는 호텔 경험들 중 하나는 / 홍콩에서였다 / 친한 친구와 함께.

우리는 머물렀다 / 작은 호텔에서 / 대중교통 근처의.

내가 처음 방에 들어갔을 때, / 나는 놀랐다 / 왜냐하면 그것이 훨씬 더 작았기 때문이다 / 내가 예상했던 것보다.

그래도, / 방은 깨끗했고, / 침대는 편안했고, / 위치는 좋았다.

우리는 쉽게 방문할 수 있었다 / 침사추이와 / 빅토리아 하버를.

낮 동안에는, / 우리는 도시를 걸어 다녔고 / 현지 음식을 먹어 봤다.

밤에는, / 우리는 빅토리아 하버에 갔고 / 도시 불빛을 즐겼다.

그 후에, / 우리는 호텔로 돌아갔고, / 간식을 먹고, / 그날에 대해 이야기했다.

호텔은 고급스럽지 않았지만, / 위치가 우리의 여행을 만들었다 / 매우 편리하게.

그것이 이유다 / 내가 아직도 그 호텔을 기억하는, / 그리고 나는 정말 다시 방문하고 싶다 / 홍콩을.

---

## Key Expressions

- smaller than I expected
- the location was great
- city lights
- go back to the hotel
- visit again
