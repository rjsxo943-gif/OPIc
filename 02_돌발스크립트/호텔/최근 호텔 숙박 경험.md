# 호텔 - 최근 호텔 숙박 경험

## Question

- Tell me about the last time you stayed at a hotel.
- Where did you stay?
- Who were you with?
- What did you do there?

---

## Speaking Order

고등학교 친구 세 명과 후쿠오카 여행

↓

호텔에 짐을 내려놓고 밖으로 나감

↓

이자카야에서 고등어회 먹음

↓

호텔에서 밤늦게 이야기함

↓

친구들 덕분에 특별했던 숙박

---

## Full Script

One of my recent hotel stays was during a trip to Fukuoka with three of my high school friends, so there were four of us in total.

We booked a hotel in a convenient area close to public transportation and many restaurants. The room was not especially large, but it was clean and comfortable enough for us.

After checking in, we dropped off our bags and immediately went outside. We walked around the city, visited shops, and looked for good places to eat.

One evening, we went to a local izakaya. We tried several Japanese dishes, and the best one was fresh koma saba, that's kind of sashimi

I had not expected raw mackerel to taste that good, so it became one of my favorite food memories from Japan.

After dinner, we headed back to the hotel. Even though we were tired, we stayed up late talking about our high school days, our current lives, and funny memories.

The room felt a little crowded because there were four of us, but that actually made the trip more enjoyable. It felt as if we had gone back to being high school students for a few days.

I do not remember every detail about the hotel facilities, but I clearly remember laughing and talking with my friends in the room.

That experience taught me that a hotel does not have to be luxurious to be memorable. The people you travel with can make an ordinary room feel special.

---

## Key Expressions

- go back to being
- make an ordinary room feel special
