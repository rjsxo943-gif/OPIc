# 롤플레이 - 문제 설명하고 대안 제시하기

## Speaking Order

문제 설명

↓

가장 원하는 해결책 제시

↓

두 번째 대안 제시

↓

마지막 대안 제시

↓

사과와 감사 인사

---

## Master Script

Hello. Could you help me with something?

I’m afraid there seems to be a problem with ______.

The problem is that ______, so I’m calling to see how we can solve this.

First of all, would it be possible to ______?

I think that would be the easiest way to fix the problem.

Oh, I see. If that’s not possible, could you ______ instead?

That would also work for me.

Well, if neither of those options works, I could ______.

It would not be my first choice, but I think it would be better than doing nothing.

I’m sorry for the trouble, and I really appreciate your help.

Please check what you can do and let me know. Thank you.

---

## 핵심 대안 연결 표현

- First of all, would it be possible to ______?
- If that’s not possible, could you ______ instead?
- Alternatively, could I ______?
- If neither of those options works, I could ______.
- That would work for me.
- It would not be my first choice, but I could accept that.

---

## 공통 반응 표현

- Oh, I see.
- That sounds good.
- That would work for me.
- I understand.
- That’s unfortunate.
- Okay, please check and let me know.

---

## Practice Checklist

- [ ] 문제를 한두 문장으로 분명하게 설명하기
- [ ] 첫 번째 대안을 가장 구체적으로 말하기
- [ ] 두 번째 대안을 `If that’s not possible`로 연결하기
- [ ] 마지막 대안을 최후의 선택처럼 말하기
- [ ] 질문 사이에 짧은 반응 넣기
- [ ] 감사나 사과로 마무리하기
