# 롤플레이 - 영화표를 잘못 구매한 상황

## Situation

영화관에서 영화표를 잘못 구매했다.

오늘 표가 아니라 내일 표가 필요하므로, 직원에게 상황을 설명하고 해결 방법을 제안한다.

---

## Speaking Order

문제 설명

↓

내일 영화표로 교환 요청

↓

교환이 어렵다면 환불 요청

↓

둘 다 어렵다면 취소 수수료를 내고 취소

↓

감사와 사과로 마무리

---

## Full Script

Excuse me. / Could you help me / with something?

I bought two movie tickets / this morning, / but I just realized / that I accidentally purchased tickets / for tonight.

The problem is / that my friend and I are supposed to watch the movie / tomorrow, / and neither of us can make it / tonight.

First of all, / would it be possible / to exchange these tickets / for the same movie tomorrow?

We would prefer / the evening show, / but any available time / would be fine.

Oh, I see. / If exchanging the tickets is not possible, / could I get / a full refund / instead?

Then I could simply buy / two new tickets / for tomorrow.

I understand.

Well, / if neither of those options works, / I would like to cancel / the tickets. / I can pay / a cancellation fee / if necessary.

That would not be my first choice, / but it would be better / than losing / the full price of the tickets.

I’m sorry / for the trouble, / and I really appreciate / your help.

Could you please check / what options are available / and let me know?

Thank you.

## 핵심 표현

- I accidentally purchased the wrong tickets.  
  : 실수로 잘못된 표를 구매했다.

- We are supposed to watch the movie tomorrow.  
  : 우리는 내일 영화를 보기로 했다.

- Neither of us can make it tonight.  
  : 우리 둘 다 오늘 저녁에는 갈 수 없다.

- Would it be possible to exchange these tickets?  
  : 이 표를 교환할 수 있을까요?

- Could I get a full refund instead?  
  : 대신 전액 환불을 받을 수 있을까요?

- If neither of those options works  
  : 그 두 방법이 모두 불가능하다면

- pay a cancellation fee  
  : 취소 수수료를 내다

- lose the full price of the tickets  
  : 티켓값 전액을 잃다

---

## 대안 구조

### 대안 1

Would it be possible to exchange these tickets for tomorrow’s show?

### 대안 2

If that’s not possible, could I get a full refund instead?

### 대안 3

If neither option works, I would like to cancel the tickets and pay the cancellation fee.

---

## 공통 반응 표현

- Oh, I see.
- I understand.
- That would work for me.
- That’s unfortunate.
- Okay, please check and let me know.

## Notes

- 핵심 순서는 `교환 → 환불 → 취소`이다.
- 영화 제목이나 시간은 굳이 구체적으로 정하지 않아도 된다.
- 표가 한 장이면 `two movie tickets`를 `a movie ticket`으로 바꾼다.
- 롤플레이에서는 해결책을 최소 두 개 이상 분명하게 제시하는 것이 중요하다.
