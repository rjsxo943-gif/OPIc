# 롤플레이 - 초대받은 파티에 관해 질문하기

## Situation

친구가 나를 주말 파티에 초대했지만, 파티에 관한 자세한 정보를 모르는 상황이다.

친구에게 전화해서 장소, 시간, 준비물 등에 관해 세 가지 또는 네 가지 질문을 한다.

---

## Speaking Order

인사와 전화 목적

↓

파티 장소 질문

↓

준비물과 음식 질문

↓

시작 시간 질문

↓

누가 오는지 추가 질문

↓

약속 확인 후 마무리

---

## Full Script

Hi, Minsoo. / It’s GeonTae.

How are you? / I’m calling / because I have a few questions / about the party / you invited me to.

First, / where is the party going to be? / Is it going to be / at your place?

Oh, it is? / Great. / I’ve never been / to your place before, / so could you send me / your address?

Okay, / thanks.

Also, / do I need to bring anything, / or can I just show up / empty-handed?

Oh, / you’re preparing / most of the food? / That sounds great.

Still, / I’d be happy to bring / some drinks or snacks. / Is there anything / you would prefer?

Sure, / I’ll bring / some beer and chips, / then.

Oh, / one more thing. / What time does the party start? / I want to make sure / I arrive on time.

Seven o’clock? / That works / for me.

By the way, / who else is coming? / Are they mostly people / I already know?

Oh, I see. / It’ll be nice / to meet some new people too.

Thanks / for letting me know. / I’m really looking forward / to the party.

I’ll see you / at your place / at seven / on Saturday. / Bye!

먼저, / 파티는 어디에서 열릴 거야? / 네 집에서 열릴 거야 / 네 집에서?

좋아, / 고마워.

## 핵심 질문

### 장소

Where is the party going to be?

Is it going to be at your place?

Could you send me your address?

### 준비물

Do I need to bring anything?

Can I just show up empty-handed?

Is there anything you would prefer?

### 시간

What time does the party start?

What time should I be there?

### 참석자

Who else is coming?

Are they mostly people I already know?

---

## 공통 반응 표현

- Oh, it is? Great.
- That sounds great.
- Sure, I can do that.
- That works for me.
- Oh, I see.
- That’s good to know.

---

## 재사용 가능한 질문

- What kind of party is it?
- Is there a dress code?
- How many people are coming?
- How long do you think the party will last?
- Should I bring some food or drinks?
- Is there somewhere nearby where I can park?
- How can I get to your place by public transportation?

## Practice Checklist

- [ ] 장소와 주소 질문하기
- [ ] 준비할 음식이나 음료 질문하기
- [ ] 시작 시간 질문하기
- [ ] 상대방의 대답을 들은 것처럼 짧게 반응하기
- [ ] 마지막에 날짜와 시간을 다시 확인하기
- [ ] 약 50초~1분 10초로 녹음하기

## Notes

- 핵심 순서는 `장소 → 준비물 → 시간 → 참석자`이다.
- 질문을 잊어버려도 장소, 시간, 준비물 세 가지만 물으면 기본 대응이 가능하다.
- `show up empty-handed`는 아무것도 가져가지 않고 나타난다는 자연스러운 표현이다.
- 친구 이름은 편하게 발음할 수 있는 이름으로 바꿔도 된다.
