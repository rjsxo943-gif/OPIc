# 해외여행 - 가장 기억에 남는 여행 경험

## Question

- Tell me about a memorable experience you had while traveling abroad.
- When and where did it happen?
- Who were you with?
- What made the experience so special?

## Full Script

One of the most memorable travel experiences I have ever had happened in Mongolia last summer.

I went there during my summer vacation with two of my college friends, so there were three of us in total. We traveled around Mongolia for six nights and seven days, and one night, we stayed in a traditional Mongolian ger.

After dinner, we went back to our ger and rested for a while. Later that night, we decided to go outside because we wanted to see the stars.

We brought out some dried mangoes, snacks, and a bottle of Mongolian vodka. We placed everything on a chair, but instead of sitting on chairs ourselves, we just sat on the ground together.

There were almost no lights around the ger camp, so the sky was incredibly clear. I could see countless stars and even several constellations. I had seen stars before, of course, but I had never seen that many at once. The sky felt much bigger than it did back home.

While looking at the stars, we drank some vodka and talked about all kinds of things. We talked about the dumplings we had eaten at a local restaurant earlier that day, and we discussed whether we preferred Chinggis Khan Gold or Eden vodka. We also talked about relationships and joked around like we normally did.

There were several large dogs living near the ger camp as well. At first, they looked a little intimidating because they were so big. However, they were actually very friendly. They stayed close to us, played with us, and seemed to be watching over the area.

What made that night so special was that we were completely focused on the trip. We were not thinking about school, work, or anything waiting for us back home. We were simply sitting under the stars, having drinks, eating snacks, and enjoying each other's company.

It was not an expensive activity or a famous tourist attraction. In fact, it was a very simple night. However, the peaceful grassland, the clear sky, my close friends, and even the dogs made it feel almost perfect.

Even now, when I think about Mongolia, that is the first night that comes to mind. It reminded me that the most memorable moments during a trip are not always the ones we plan in advance.

Sometimes, an ordinary moment becomes unforgettable because of the people you are with and the atmosphere around you.

## IH Points

### 1. 시간과 장소가 분명함

작년 여름방학, 대학 친구 두 명과 떠난 6박 7일 몽골 여행 중 게르에서 보낸 밤으로 상황을 명확하게 설정했다.

### 2. 하나의 구체적인 장면에 집중함

여행 전체를 나열하지 않고, 친구들과 땅바닥에 앉아 별을 보며 보드카를 마신 한 장면을 중심으로 이야기했다.

### 3. 개인적인 디테일이 풍부함

건망고, 과자, 몽골 만두, 징기스칸 골드와 에덴 보드카, 연애 이야기 등을 사용해 실제 경험처럼 생생하게 구성했다.

### 4. 예상 밖의 요소를 넣음

처음에는 무섭게 보였던 커다란 개들이 실제로는 친근했고, 곁에서 놀아주며 주변을 지켜줬다는 내용을 넣었다.

### 5. 경험의 의미로 마무리함

유명 관광지가 아니라 평범한 순간이 사람과 분위기 덕분에 가장 특별한 기억이 되었다고 정리했다.
