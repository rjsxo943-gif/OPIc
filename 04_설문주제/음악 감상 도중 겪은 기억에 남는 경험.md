## Full Script

One of the most memorable experiences I've ever had with music happened at my university festival last fall.

Beenzino, who has been my favorite Korean rapper since I was young, came to perform at the festival. As soon as I heard that he was coming, I became really excited. Some of my close friends also liked his music, so we decided to go to the concert together.

On the day of the festival, we headed over to the outdoor stage a little early because we wanted to get a good spot. There were already a lot of students waiting for the performance, and everyone seemed excited. Music was playing through the speakers, people were talking loudly, and the whole place was full of energy.

When Beenzino finally came out on stage, the crowd went wild. My friends and I shouted, sang along, and recorded a few short videos on our phones. He performed several of his popular songs, including **Dali, Van, Picasso** and **Aqua Man**.

The most memorable moment was when he started performing **Aqua Man**. It was the first song that really got me into his music when I was younger, so hearing it live felt completely different from listening to it through my earphones.

As soon as the song started, almost everyone in the audience began singing along. I could hear thousands of people singing the same lyrics together, and the energy was incredible. I even got goosebumps because the song brought back so many memories from my younger days.

At that moment, I looked around at my friends and realized that we were all singing and enjoying the same song together. It felt as if I had gone back in time for a moment. Honestly, it felt like one of the songs from my youth had suddenly come to life right in front of me.

Before that experience, I had mostly listened to Beenzino's music through my AirPods or speakers. I already loved his songs, but seeing him perform live made me appreciate his music even more. His voice, the loud beat, the stage lights, and the reaction of the audience created an atmosphere that I could never experience by listening to a recording alone.

Even now, whenever I listen to **Aqua Man**, I sometimes think back to that night at my university festival. It was not just a concert to me. It felt like a special moment from my youth that I got to share with my close friends.

That's why it remains one of my most memorable music-related experiences, and I would definitely love to see him perform live again someday.

## IH Points

### 1. 특정 경험을 명확하게 제시함

대학교 축제에서 친구들과 빈지노의 공연을 본 경험으로 시간, 장소, 인물을 구체적으로 설명했다.

### 2. 현장 분위기를 묘사함

학생들, 스피커에서 나오는 음악, 관객들의 함성, 무대 조명 등을 사용해 당시 상황이 그려지도록 만들었다.

### 3. 가장 기억에 남는 순간을 강조함

단순히 공연이 재미있었다고 끝내지 않고, 어릴 때부터 좋아한 `Aqua Man`을 라이브로 들었던 순간을 중심 사건으로 잡았다.

### 4. 과거의 기억과 현재의 감정을 연결함

노래를 들으며 어린 시절의 기억이 떠올랐고, 현재도 그 노래를 들으면 축제의 밤이 생각난다고 연결했다.

### 5. 음악의 의미를 개인적으로 표현함

공연을 단순한 콘서트가 아니라 친구들과 함께한 청춘의 특별한 순간으로 표현했다.
