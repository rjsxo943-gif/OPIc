# Full Script

I live in a small studio apartment by myself.

I've lived here for about three years, and I have to say I really like where I live, although it is a small place.

Since it is a studio apartment, I don't really have many separate rooms.

Everything is in one open space, like my bed, desk, small kitchen area, and living area.

So, if I had to choose my favorite part of my home, I would say it is the area around my bed.

It is not very big, but it is the most comfortable place in my apartment.

I have a nice comfortable bed, a small desk, and a thick beige rug on the floor.

Also, I put my bed near the window, so I can get some fresh air when I open it.

When I lie down on my bed after a long day, I feel really relaxed.

I also like spending time there watching YouTube, reading books, or just resting.

I don't have a lot of furniture in my place because I prefer to live with only the things I need.

In the kitchen area, there are some appliances like a fridge, a microwave, and a stove.

Overall, my home is always the best place for me to relax.

It is small, but it is cozy and comfortable.

For me, it feels like my safe haven.
