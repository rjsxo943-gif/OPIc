---

## SELF-1 — 학생과 현재 진로

**장면:** 인사 → 이름과 나이 → 전자공학 4학년 → 취업 준비

Hi Eva, nice to meet you.

I'm doing pretty well, but honestly, I'm a little nervous right now. Still, I'll try my best to answer your questions.

My name is GeonTae Kim. I don't have an English name, so you can just call me GeonTae. I'm 25 years old.

I'm currently a senior majoring in electronic engineering, and I'm expected to graduate soon. So these days, I'm preparing to get a job as an engineer.

---

## SELF-2 — 전공 관심과 졸업 프로젝트

**장면:** 취업 때문에 전공 선택 → 잘 맞는다는 발견 → 스마트팩토리 → SDR 드론 탐지

To be honest, I first chose electronic engineering because I thought it would give me good job opportunities, especially at big companies.

But after studying it for several years, I realized that this field actually suits me pretty well. These days, I'm interested in smart factories and automation systems.

Recently, I worked on my graduation project. It was about detecting drone signals using SDR.

Through that project, I learned how to collect signal data, analyze it, and build a real-time detection system. It was challenging, but I felt proud because I could connect what I learned in school with a real engineering problem.

---

## SELF-3 — 성격과 취미

**장면:** 혼자도 좋아함 → 사람 만나는 것도 좋아함 → 웃김·책임감 → 운동·여행·맛집

As for my personality, I like spending time alone, but I also enjoy meeting people.

My close friends usually say I'm funny and responsible, and I think that's pretty accurate.

When I have free time, I like working out, traveling, and visiting good restaurants. Working out helps me relieve stress, and I like the feeling of focusing on myself.

When I stay at home, I usually watch YouTube, read books, or talk with my family.

---

## SELF-4 — 미래의 모습

**장면:** 인정받는 엔지니어 → 따뜻한 가족 구성원 → 새로운 인생 시작

In the future, I want to become a capable engineer who is recognized by others.

At the same time, I also want to be a warm-hearted son, husband, and father.

So I guess that's everything about me. I'm really looking forward to starting a new chapter in my life.
