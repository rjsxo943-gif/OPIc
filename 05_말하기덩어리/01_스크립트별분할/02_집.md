# 2. 좋아하는 공간 — 침대 주변

> 원본: [[01_설문스크립트/집/좋아하는 공간 - 침대 주변|좋아하는 공간 - 침대 주변]]

## HOME-FAV-1 — 집 구조와 좋아하는 공간 선택

**장면:** 혼자 스튜디오 거주 → 3년 → 별도 방 없음 → 침대 주변 선택

I live alone in a small studio apartment, and I've been living there for about three years.

Since it is a studio apartment, everything is in one open space, so technically, I do not have separate rooms.

Still, if I had to choose my favorite part of my home, it would definitely be the corner around my bed.

## HOME-FAV-2 — 침대 주변의 배치와 분위기

**장면:** 창문 옆 침대 → 작은 책상 → 베이지 러그 → 신선한 공기

My bed is right next to the window, and I also have a small desk and a thick beige rug on the floor.

It is not a large space, but it feels very comfortable and cozy.

When I open the window, fresh air comes in, which makes the whole area feel calm and refreshing.

## HOME-FAV-3 — 그곳에서 하는 일

**장면:** 유튜브 → 독서 → 아무것도 안 하고 쉬기 → 대부분의 자유시간

After a long day, I usually lie down on my bed and watch YouTube.

Sometimes, I read a book at my desk, or I just rest without doing anything.

Because I live alone, I spend most of my free time in that little corner. It is where I can forget about school, work, and everything else for a while.

## HOME-FAV-4 — 단순하지만 나만의 안식처

**장면:** 가구가 많지 않음 → 평화로움 → 남에게 평범하지만 나에게 특별함

I do not have a lot of furniture because I prefer to keep the things I really need.

So, the area around my bed feels peaceful and easy to relax in.

It may not look very special to other people, but to me, it feels like my own safe haven. That is why the corner around my bed is my favorite place in my home.

# 3. 과거 집과 현재 집 비교

> 원본: [[01_설문스크립트/집/과거집비교|과거 집과 현재 집 비교]]

## HOME-COMP-1 — 과거 집과 독립

**장면:** 가족과 넓은 집 → 성장한 곳 → 3년 전 독립 → 비교 시작

When I was young, I lived with my family in a spacious apartment.

It was the place where I grew up and spent my teenage years. But I moved out of my parents' home about three years ago.

So let me compare the home I lived in before with my current home.

## HOME-COMP-2 — 과거 가족 집의 모습

**장면:** 여러 방과 욕실 → 큰 거실 → 가족이 함께함 → 밝은 창문

In the past, I lived in a large apartment with my family.

It had several bedrooms, two bathrooms, and a large living room. There was also a kitchen and a dining area, so my family often spent time together there.

Also, the living room had big windows, so it was very bright during the day.

## HOME-COMP-3 — 현재 스튜디오의 모습

**장면:** 훨씬 작음 → 혼자 거주 → 모든 것이 한 공간 → 필요한 것만 둠

In contrast, my current home is much smaller. Now, I live in a small studio apartment by myself.

Unlike my parents' home, everything is in one open space, like my bed, desk, small kitchen area, and living area.

So I don't have a lot of furniture. I only keep the things I really need, like my bed, desk, and some kitchen appliances.

## HOME-COMP-4 — 현재 집을 더 좋아하는 이유

**장면:** 작지만 조용하고 아늑함 → 내 공간 → 방해 없이 활동 → 안식처

But on the plus side, my current apartment is very cozy and quiet. Also, I really like having my own space.

I can study, relax, watch YouTube, or read books without being disturbed.

Overall, I'm quite satisfied with where I live now. It is small, but it is comfortable, and for me, it feels like a safe haven.

# 4. 집에서 생긴 문제

> 원본: [[01_설문스크립트/집/집문제|집에서 생긴 문제]]

## HOME-PROBLEM-1 — 욕실 문제 발견

**장면:** 큰 문제는 별로 없음 → 토요일 아침 → 욕실 바닥의 물 → 변기 누수 발견

Honestly, I haven't had many serious problems with my home.

But now that I think about it, I remember a time when I had a problem in my bathroom.

One Saturday morning, I woke up and found that the bathroom floor was covered with water. At first, I was really surprised because I didn't know where the water was coming from.

So I checked the bathroom carefully and noticed that water was leaking from the toilet.

## HOME-PROBLEM-2 — 혼자 해결하지 못해 도움 요청

**장면:** 혼자라 스트레스 → 고칠 줄 모름 → 배관공 호출 → 한 시간 대기

I got really stressed because I live alone, and I didn't know how to fix it by myself.

So I called a plumber right away and explained the situation.

Unfortunately, he couldn't come immediately, so I had to wait for about an hour.

## HOME-PROBLEM-3 — 수리와 청소

**장면:** 배관공 도착 → 빠르게 수리 → 남은 물 청소 → 오래 걸림

When the plumber finally arrived, he checked the toilet and fixed the leak pretty quickly.

The problem was solved, but I still had to clean up all the water on the bathroom floor.

I remember it took quite a long time to clean everything up.

## HOME-PROBLEM-4 — 혼자 사는 삶에 대한 교훈

**장면:** 매우 스트레스 → 혼자 사는 편안함의 반대편 → 예상 밖 문제를 직접 처리

Overall, it was such a stressful experience.

After that day, I realized that living alone is comfortable, but sometimes I have to deal with unexpected problems by myself.

# 5. 집에 준 변화

> 원본: [[01_설문스크립트/집/집변화|집에 준 변화]]

## HOME-CHANGE-1 — 작은 집이라 작은 변화만 함

**장면:** 큰 변화는 어려움 → 침대 주변 중심 → 새 침대로 수면 개선

I haven't made huge changes to my home because my studio apartment is not that big.

But I have made some small changes to make it more comfortable. The biggest change was around my bed.

A while ago, I bought a new bed because my old bed was not comfortable. After changing my bed, I started sleeping much better, so I think it was a really good decision.

## HOME-CHANGE-2 — 러그와 가구 재배치

**장면:** 베이지 러그 → 따뜻하고 아늑함 → 침대와 책상을 창문 쪽으로 이동

I also bought a thick beige rug and put it on the floor. It made my apartment feel much warmer and cozier.

Another small change was that I rearranged some furniture. I moved my bed and desk a little closer to the window.

Now, I can sit there, drink coffee, watch YouTube, or read books while getting some sunlight.

## HOME-CHANGE-3 — 작은 변화가 만든 결과

**장면:** 큰 변화는 아니었음 → 집이 훨씬 좋아짐 → 내 공간·안식처가 됨

Overall, these were not huge changes, but they made my home much better.

Now my apartment feels more like my own place.

It is small, but it is cozy, comfortable, and it has become my safe haven.
