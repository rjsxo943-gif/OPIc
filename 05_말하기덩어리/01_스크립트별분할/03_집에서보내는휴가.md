# 집에서 보내는 휴가 — 큰 덩어리 분할

# 1. 평소 활동 경향

> 원본: [[01_설문스크립트/집에서보내는휴가/집에서 보내는 휴가 - 평소 활동 경향|평소 활동 경향]]

## VAC-HABIT-1 — 집에서 쉬는 휴가를 좋아하는 이유

**장면:** 여행도 좋아함 → 지치면 집이 최고 → 혼자 원하는 방식대로 하루를 보냄

I actually enjoy spending some of my vacations at home.

I like traveling and going out with friends, but when I feel physically or mentally tired, staying at home is the best way for me to recover.

Since I usually live alone, I can spend the entire day exactly the way I want without worrying about anyone else.

## VAC-HABIT-2 — 느긋한 아침과 혼자 보는 콘텐츠

**장면:** 오전 10시 기상 → 물·세안·아침 → 유튜브·웹툰 → 여행 영상

On a typical day off, I wake up at around ten in the morning. The first thing I do is drink a large glass of water, probably about four hundred milliliters. Then I wash my face and have a simple breakfast.

After that, I usually lie down and watch YouTube or read webtoons. I especially enjoy travel channels like Pani Bottle and KwakTube.

Watching their videos allows me to experience different countries without actually leaving my room.

## VAC-HABIT-3 — 독서·완전한 휴식·가족과 음식

**장면:** 관심 있는 책 → 생산성 강요하지 않음 → 혼자 먹거나 가족과 치킨·피자

When I have a book that I am interested in, I also spend some time reading. I normally read on my bed or couch while listening to quiet music.

Unlike regular weekdays, I do not force myself to exercise or do anything productive. I prefer to completely relax and give my body a break.

When I am alone, I usually eat whatever I already have at home. However, when I stay at my parents' place and my family is around, we sometimes order something delicious, usually chicken or pizza, and eat it together.

## VAC-HABIT-4 — 답답할 때의 외출과 휴가의 의미

**장면:** 오래 있으면 산책·카페 → 돈 절약 → 밀린 콘텐츠 → 재충전

If I start to feel bored or uncomfortable after staying indoors for too long, I go out for a short walk. Sometimes, I take a book to a nearby café and read there for a while.

That gives me a change of scenery without making the day too busy.

What I like most about a stay-at-home vacation is that I do not have to spend much money. I can stay in a cool and comfortable home, enjoy some time alone, and catch up on all the YouTube videos and webtoons I have been saving.

For me, a good vacation does not always have to involve traveling somewhere. Sometimes, staying home and doing almost nothing is exactly what I need to recharge.

# 2. 기억에 남는 휴가

> 원본: [[01_설문스크립트/집에서보내는휴가/집에서 보내는 휴가 - 기억에 남는 휴가|기억에 남는 휴가]]

## VAC-MEM-1 — 시험이 끝난 뒤 완전히 자유로웠던 날

**장면:** 기말 종료 다음 월요일 → 본가 → 수업·마감·계획 없음 → 오랜만의 자유

The most memorable stay-at-home vacation I can remember was the Monday after my final tests ended.

I had just finished a stressful semester and returned to my parents' home.

I did not have any classes, deadlines, or plans that day, so for the first time in a long while, I felt completely free.

## VAC-MEM-2 — 죄책감 없이 빈둥거리며 콘텐츠 보기

**장면:** 늦잠 → 혼자 누워 있음 → 공부 걱정 없음 → 유튜브·웹툰 → 유럽 신혼여행 상상

I woke up late and spent most of the day lying around at home by myself.

Normally, I would feel a little guilty about doing nothing, but that day was different. Since my tests were finally over, I could relax without constantly thinking that I should be studying.

I caught up on YouTube videos and webtoons that I had saved for later. One video I particularly remember was a honeymoon video by KwakTube.

He was traveling around Europe, and while I was watching it, I started thinking that I would love to go on a honeymoon in Europe someday. It was nothing serious, but it was nice to lie in bed and imagine my future without worrying about anything happening at that moment.

## VAC-MEM-3 — 가족과 보낸 평범한 저녁

**장면:** 가족 퇴근 → 치킨과 피자 → 평범한 이야기 → 함께 있는 시간

Later in the evening, my family came home from work.

We ordered chicken and pizza and ate together. We talked about ordinary things, watched some videos, and simply enjoyed being together.

What made that vacation so memorable was not that I did anything exciting. In fact, I barely did anything at all. The special part was the feeling of having absolutely nothing hanging over me.

## VAC-MEM-4 — 지금 더 특별하게 느껴지는 이유

**장면:** 하루 종일 평화 → 현재는 취업 준비 압박 → 다시 그런 휴가를 원함

My tests were over, I was back home with my family, and I had enough time to enjoy all the things I had put off. The whole day felt peaceful from beginning to end.

These days, I have an important goal of finding a job, so even when I take a break, part of my mind is usually thinking about what I need to do next. That is probably why I remember that day so clearly.

It may have been one of the last times I could rest without feeling any pressure.

I would definitely love to spend another vacation like that. Realistically, it might not happen until after I get a job, but when that day comes, I want to lie down, eat good food with my family, and enjoy doing absolutely nothing again.

# 3. 휴가 관련 이슈

> 원본: [[01_설문스크립트/집에서보내는휴가/집에서 보내는 휴가 - 휴가 관련 이슈|휴가 관련 이슈]]

## VAC-ISSUE-1 — 사람들이 휴가를 필요로 하는 이유

**장면:** 일·공부·책임 → 스트레스 뒤 휴식 → 머리 비우기·피로 회복·가족

To be honest, I think people need vacations for many different reasons.

Most people spend a lot of time working, studying, or dealing with responsibilities.

After finishing something stressful, they need some time to clear their minds, recover from their exhaustion, and spend time with family members they have not seen very often.

## VAC-ISSUE-2 — 활동적인 휴가와 회복형 휴가

**장면:** 여행·활동·친구 → 시간을 잘 썼다는 느낌 / 집에서 늦잠·TV·음식·혼자 회복

However, people do not all spend their vacations in the same way.

Some people prefer to fill their schedules with trips, activities, and meetings with friends. They want to visit new places, try new food, and create special memories. For them, staying busy during a vacation makes them feel that they have used their time well.

On the other hand, some people prefer to stay at home and do almost nothing. They may sleep late, watch TV, order good food, or simply enjoy some time alone. I think this can also be a very good vacation, especially when someone is physically or mentally exhausted.

## VAC-ISSUE-3 — 현재 나에게 필요한 휴가

**장면:** 둘 다 좋아함 → 기억이 필요하면 여행 → 지금은 공부·부트캠프·취업 준비 → 회복이 더 중요

Personally, I enjoy both kinds of vacations. When I want to make new memories, I like traveling or meeting my friends. However, when I am tired and need to recover, I would rather stay at home and relax.

At this point in my life, I think a quiet vacation at home is more important for me. I have been busy with studying, a boot camp, and preparing for my future career.

Even if I get a day off, I may spend a little time reviewing what I have learned or checking my progress. Still, I need enough time to rest because when I do not rest properly, I cannot concentrate and my efficiency drops.

## VAC-ISSUE-4 — 목적에 맞는 휴가가 좋은 휴가

**장면:** 하나의 정답 없음 → 경험이 필요하면 여행 → 회복이 필요하면 집 → 일상으로 돌아갈 힘

So, in my opinion, there is no single perfect way to spend a vacation.

If someone needs new experiences, traveling may be the best choice. If someone needs recovery, staying home may be better.

The most important thing is to spend a vacation in a way that matches your purpose.

A good vacation should help you return to your daily life feeling refreshed and ready to move forward again.
