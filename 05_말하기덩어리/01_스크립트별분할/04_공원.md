# 공원 — 큰 덩어리 분할

# 1. 좋아하는 공원

> 원본: [[01_설문스크립트/공원/좋아하는 공원|좋아하는 공원]]

## PARK-FAV-1 — 공원을 좋아하고 자주 가는 이유

**장면:** 부산의 여러 공원 → 봄에 더 자주 감 → 북항친수공원 소개

I really like going to parks, so I've been to a lot of beautiful parks in my country.

Actually, I live in a big city, Busan, but there are several nice parks nearby.

So I go to the park at least once or twice a week, and often a lot more during the spring.

Speaking of the park I like to go to, there is a park called North Port Waterfront Park right behind Busan Station.

## PARK-FAV-2 — 위치와 접근성

**장면:** 집에서 걸어갈 수 있음 → 도보 15분 → 정기적으로 방문하기 편함

Fortunately, it's located within walking distance of my home.

It takes about fifteen minutes to get there on foot, so it's very convenient and easy for me to visit regularly.

## PARK-FAV-3 — 시설과 봄 정원

**장면:** 산책로·벤치·운동 시설 → 가장 좋아하는 정원 → 꽃과 향기

In addition, what makes North Port Waterfront Park special is that it has some great facilities.

There are walking trails all over the park, and there are plenty of benches where people can sit down and relax.

On top of that, the park has everything you would expect, like soccer fields, basketball courts, and beautiful gardens.

The gardens are my favorite part of the park because the flowers begin to blossom every spring. I mean, there are so many flowers and plants, and they give off an incredible scent.

## PARK-FAV-4 — 도심 속 자연과 마무리

**장면:** 도심인데 시골처럼 느낌 → 가까운 공원에 감사 → 친구·가족과 즐기기 좋음

So whenever I go to North Port Waterfront Park, it always feels like I'm out in the countryside even though I'm right in the middle of a big city.

Overall, I feel very lucky to have such a beautiful park close to my home.

I would say North Port Waterfront Park is an excellent place to hang out with friends or family.

Although the park gets quite crowded sometimes, it's a perfect place to enjoy nature.

# 2. 공원에서 하는 활동

> 원본: [[01_설문스크립트/공원/공원에서 하는 활동|공원에서 하는 활동]]

## PARK-HABIT-1 — 가까운 공원과 방문 빈도

**장면:** 동네의 예쁜 공원 → 도보 15분 → 일주일에 두 번 이상

There are quite a few pretty parks in my city.

Like I said in the previous question, an especially nice one is only about fifteen minutes from where I live.

The park in my neighborhood is called North Port Waterfront Park, and I really like going there to spend some time in nature.

So I go to the park at least a couple of times a week.

## PARK-HABIT-2 — 혼자 새벽에 가는 이유

**장면:** 새벽 운동을 좋아함 → 친구들은 5시 30분을 싫어함 → 봄 산책은 가족·친구와

I usually go alone because I like to exercise at the crack of dawn.

I wouldn't mind going with friends, but nobody wants a wake-up call at 5:30 in the morning.

However, when I just want to take a walk during the spring, I usually go with my friends or family.

## PARK-HABIT-3 — 러닝 중심 운동 루틴

**장면:** 주 활동은 러닝 → 3km·15분 → 운동기구 → 스트레칭·쿨다운

The main activity I do at the park is running.

The park is perfect for running because it has a nice running path.

My usual routine is to run about three kilometers, which takes around fifteen minutes.

If I still have enough energy, I use the outdoor workout machines. After that, I stretch my arms and legs.

Finally, I cool down and usually grab an energy drink.

## PARK-HABIT-4 — 공원 운동을 좋아하는 이유

**장면:** 건강하고 상쾌함 → 러닝머신보다 자연 → 건강 유지에 좋은 방법

I love working out at the park because I always feel healthy and refreshed afterwards.

Personally, I'd much rather be out in nature than run on a treadmill.

All in all, I think going to the park is one of the best ways to stay healthy.

# 3. 공원에서 겪은 기억에 남는 경험

> 원본: [[01_설문스크립트/공원/공원에서 겪은 기억에 남는 경험|공원 경험]]

## PARK-MEM-1 — 가을 축제의 즐거운 분위기

**장면:** 지난가을 → 혼자 축제 방문 → 푸드트럭·부스·라이브 음악 → 즐거움

One of the most memorable experiences I had at this park happened last fall.

There was a small weekend festival at the park, so I decided to go there alone to relax and enjoy the atmosphere.

There were several food trucks, small booths, and live music performances throughout the park.

A lot of people were walking around, eating delicious food, and taking pictures. The atmosphere was lively, and I was having a great time.

## PARK-MEM-2 — 옛 벤치와 전 여자친구

**장면:** 예전에 함께 앉던 벤치 → 추억 → 다른 남자와 있는 전 여자친구 발견

While I was walking around, I decided to visit a bench that I used to sit on with my ex-girlfriend.

When we were dating, we often came to this park together and usually sat on the same bench.

However, as I walked toward it, I suddenly saw my ex-girlfriend sitting there with another guy.

## PARK-MEM-3 — 충격과 민망함

**장면:** 예상하지 못함 → 완전 충격 → 편한 옷차림 → 모른 척 떠남

I really didn't expect to see her, so I was completely shocked.

To make things worse, I was wearing very casual clothes because I hadn't planned to meet anyone.

I felt extremely embarrassed and awkward.

So I pretended not to see them and quickly walked away.

## PARK-MEM-4 — 당시와 지금의 감정 차이

**장면:** 당시에는 불편함 → 지금은 웃김 → 잊을 수 없는 기억

At the time, the situation was very uncomfortable.

However, when I look back on it now, I actually think it was pretty funny.

That's why it has become one of my most unforgettable memories at the park.
