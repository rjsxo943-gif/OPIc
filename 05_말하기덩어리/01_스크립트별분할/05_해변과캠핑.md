# 1. 좋아하는 해변

> 원본: [[01_설문스크립트/해변과캠핑/좋아하는 해변|좋아하는 해변]]

## BEACH-FAV-1 — 부산과 광안리 소개

**장면:** 부산 출신 → 여러 해변 → 광안리가 가장 좋음 → 어릴 때부터 방문

That's a good question because I'm originally from Busan, and I've been to many beaches there.

Among them, my favorite beach is Gwangalli Beach.

It's one of the most popular beaches in Busan, and I've been going there since I was young.

## BEACH-FAV-2 — 위치·이동·동행

**장면:** 수영구 → 버스 30분 → 친구와 방문 → 계획 없이 가기 쉬움

Gwangalli Beach is located in Suyeong-gu, Busan.

It's not very far from my home, so it usually takes me about 30 minutes to get there by bus.

I normally go there with my friends, especially when the weather is nice.

Since it's easy to get there by public transportation, we can visit the beach without making any special plans.

## BEACH-FAV-3 — 낮에 하는 활동과 분위기

**장면:** 긴 모래사장·파도 소리 → 돗자리·간식 → 벤치·산책 → 낮과 저녁의 차이

What I like most about Gwangalli Beach is its beautiful scenery.

It has a long sandy beach, and I really enjoy listening to the sound of the waves.

During the daytime, my friends and I usually spread out a picnic mat on the sand and sit there while talking and eating snacks.

Sometimes, we sit on a bench or take a long walk along the beach.

I enjoy both the lively atmosphere during the day and the peaceful atmosphere later in the evening.

## BEACH-FAV-4 — 일몰·광안대교 야경·스트레스 해소

**장면:** 일몰보다 더 인상적인 야경 → 광안대교 → 도시와 바다 분위기 → 머리 비우기

The sunset is beautiful, but the night view is even more impressive because you can see Gwangandaegyo Bridge shining over the water.

All in all, Gwangalli Beach is my favorite place because I can enjoy both a lively city atmosphere and a relaxing beach atmosphere.

Whether I'm walking along the beach or sitting on a picnic mat, I always have a great time there.

Going to Gwangalli Beach is one of the best ways for me to clear my head and relieve stress.

# 2. 해변에 간 기억에 남는 경험

> 원본: [[01_설문스크립트/해변과캠핑/해변을 가는 경향|해변을 가는 경향]]

## BEACH-MEM-1 — 힘들어하던 친구와 즉흥 출발

**장면:** 지난여름 → 친구가 스트레스 → 광안리 즉흥 여행 → 늦은 오후 버스

One of my most memorable trips to the beach happened last summer.

One of my close friends was feeling stressed, so we decided to go to Gwangalli Beach together.

It was a spontaneous trip, and we took a bus there in the late afternoon.

## BEACH-MEM-2 — 좋은 날씨와 돗자리 대화

**장면:** 맑은 하늘·시원한 바람 → 활기 있지만 붐비지 않음 → 돗자리·간식·음악·대화

When we arrived, the weather was perfect.

The sky was clear, and there was a cool breeze coming from the ocean. The beach was quite lively, but it wasn't too crowded.

We found a good spot on the sand and spread out a picnic mat.

We bought some drinks and snacks from a nearby convenience store and sat on the mat for a while. We talked about our lives, listened to music, and watched people walking along the beach.

## BEACH-MEM-3 — 산책·일몰·광안대교 야경

**장면:** 물가 산책 → 일몰 사진 → 다리 조명 → 벤치에서 파도 들으며 대화

Later, we took a long walk near the water and took lots of pictures of the sunset.

As it got darker, Gwangandaegyo Bridge lit up, and the night view was amazing.

We sat on a bench, listened to the sound of the waves, and continued talking for a long time.

## BEACH-MEM-4 — 친구의 감정 변화와 의미

**장면:** 친구가 편안해짐 → 비싸거나 특별하지 않음 → 걱정을 잊음 → 지금도 스트레스 해소 장소

My friend seemed much happier and more relaxed than before.

Looking back, it wasn't an expensive or unusual trip, but it became a very meaningful memory for me.

We were able to forget about our worries and enjoy the beautiful scenery together.

That's why going to Gwangalli Beach is still one of my favorite ways to clear my head and relieve stress.

# 3. 해변 캠핑에 가져가는 물건

> 원본: [[01_설문스크립트/해변과캠핑/해변에 가져가는 물건|해변에 가져가는 물건]]

## CAMP-ITEM-1 — 여행 성격과 기본 장비

**장면:** 친구 3명과 1박 → 필요한 것만 챙기려 하지만 많음 → 텐트·침낭·의자·테이블

When I go camping near the beach, I usually go with three of my close friends.

Since we normally stay for one night, we try to bring only the things we really need.

However, we still end up packing quite a lot because we need camping equipment, clothes, food, and drinks.

The most important things we bring are a tent, sleeping bags, camping chairs, and a small table.

## CAMP-ITEM-2 — 개인 물품·요리 장비·음식

**장면:** 옷·수건·운동화·수영복 → 버너·그릴 → 고기·채소·라면·음료

We also pack a change of clothes, towels, and comfortable shoes. Since the campsite is close to the beach, we sometimes bring swimsuits as well.

For cooking, we bring a portable camping stove, a small grill, and some basic cooking tools.

As for food, we usually pack meat, vegetables, instant noodles, snacks, and plenty of drinks.

## CAMP-ITEM-3 — 기타 필수품과 준비의 가치

**장면:** 물·선크림·모기퇴치제·충전기·스피커 → 네 명이 나눔 → 편안한 여행

We also make sure to bring bottled water, sunscreen, insect repellent, and a portable charger.

Oh, and one more thing—we always bring a Bluetooth speaker because we enjoy listening to music while eating and talking.

Now that I think about it, we bring quite a lot of stuff even for a short camping trip.

Still, every item is useful, and sharing the equipment among four people makes it much easier to carry and set up.

So, I would say being well prepared makes our beach camping trips much more comfortable and enjoyable.

# 4. 캠핑 간 경험

> 원본: [[01_설문스크립트/해변과캠핑/캠핑 간 경험|캠핑 간 경험]]

## CAMP-MEM-1 — 친구들과 송정으로 출발

**장면:** 올해 여름 → 친구 3명 → 1박 2일 → 스트레스 해소 → 송정 → 차로 1시간

The last time I went camping was earlier this summer.

I went on a two-day, one-night camping trip with three of my close friends.

We had all been busy and stressed, so we decided to spend a weekend at a campsite near Songjeong Beach in Busan.

The campsite was located close to the beach, so we could easily walk to the ocean. It took us about an hour to get there by car.

## CAMP-MEM-2 — 캠핑장 분위기와 텐트 설치

**장면:** 따뜻하고 맑음·시원한 바람 → 조용한 캠핑장 → 텐트가 어려워 네 명 협력

The weather was warm and sunny, and there was a cool breeze coming from the ocean.

The campsite wasn't too crowded, so the atmosphere was peaceful and relaxing.

When we arrived, the first thing we did was set up our tent, chairs, and table.

Honestly, pitching the tent was harder than we expected, so all four of us had to work together.

## CAMP-MEM-3 — 해변 산책과 저녁·밤

**장면:** 산책·사진·파도 → 고기와 채소 → 일몰 → 음악과 긴 대화

After setting everything up, we walked along the beach, took some pictures, and listened to the sound of the waves.

In the evening, we cooked meat and vegetables on a small camping grill.

We ate dinner while watching the sunset, and the view was absolutely beautiful.

Later that night, we sat outside the tent, listened to music, and talked about our lives for hours.

## CAMP-MEM-4 — 다음 날과 여행의 의미

**장면:** 아침·마지막 산책 → 짧지만 기억에 남음 → 친구·파도 소리 → 다시 가고 싶음

The next morning, we woke up early, had a simple breakfast, and took one last walk along the beach before heading home.

Looking back, it was only a short trip, but it became one of my most memorable camping experiences.

I really enjoyed spending time with my friends and falling asleep to the sound of the waves.

The trip helped all of us forget about our stress, so I'd definitely like to go camping there again.
