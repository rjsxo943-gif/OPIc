# 1. 좋아하는 가수

> 원본: [[01_설문스크립트/음악/좋아하는 가수 (Standard Script)|좋아하는 가수]]

## MUSIC-FAV-1 — 빈지노 소개

**장면:** 어릴 때부터 한국 힙합 → 한 명을 고르면 빈지노 → 오랫동안 좋아함

That's a great question.

I've been really into Korean hip-hop since I was young, and if I had to choose just one artist, it'd definitely be Beenzino.

He's a Korean rapper, and I've been listening to his music for years. Even now, he's still my favorite artist.

## MUSIC-FAV-2 — 좋아하는 음악 스타일

**장면:** 독특한 목소리 → 세련된 랩 → 재즈 힙합과 현대 힙합 → 질리지 않음

What I like most about him is his unique voice and his polished rap style.

I also love how he mixes jazz hip-hop with modern hip-hop.

His music always sounds fresh, so I never get tired of listening to it.

## MUSIC-FAV-3 — 24:26과 Aqua Man

**장면:** 좋아하는 앨범 → 청춘과 일상 가사 → 공감 → 입문곡 Aqua Man

My favorite album is **24:26**. I've listened to every song on that album many times because the lyrics really resonate with me.

They talk about youth and everyday life, so I can relate to them.

The first song that really got me into his music was **Aqua Man**. I remember listening to it over and over again because the beat was so catchy.

After that, I started checking out his other songs, and that's how I became a huge fan of his music.

## MUSIC-FAV-4 — 언제 듣는지와 개인적 의미

**장면:** 운동·운전·산책 → 기분 전환 → 청춘의 사운드트랙

I usually put on his music while I'm working out, driving, or taking a walk.

It helps me relax, and it always puts me in a good mood.

If I had to describe Beenzino in one sentence, I'd say his music has been the soundtrack to my youth.

That's why he'll probably always be my favorite artist.

# 2. 음악 감상 활동 경향

> 원본: [[01_설문스크립트/음악/음악 감상 활동 경향|음악 감상 활동 경향]]

## MUSIC-HABIT-1 — 활동에 따라 음악을 선택함

**장면:** 음악은 일상의 큰 부분 → 한국 힙합과 클래식 → 상황에 따라 선택

I absolutely love listening to music, and I would say that it has become a big part of my daily routine.

My taste in music is fairly broad, but these days, I mostly listen to Korean hip-hop and classical music.

The kind of music I choose usually depends on what I am doing at the time.

## MUSIC-HABIT-2 — 운동할 때는 랩

**장면:** 운동할 때 가장 자주 들음 → 강한 비트 → 에너지·동작 집중 → 지칠 때 계속하게 함

I listen to music most often when I am working out.

When I exercise, I usually put on rap music, especially energetic Korean hip-hop songs.

The strong beats give me energy and help me stay focused on each movement. They also help me keep going when I start to feel tired, so music is almost essential whenever I work out.

## MUSIC-HABIT-3 — 공부와 독서에는 클래식

**장면:** 가사가 방해됨 → 가사 없는 클래식 → 마음을 맑게 하고 오래 집중

On the other hand, when I am studying or reading a book, I prefer listening to classical music without any lyrics.

Songs with lyrics can sometimes distract me because I end up paying attention to the words.

Classical music is much calmer, so it helps me clear my mind and concentrate for a longer period of time.

Besides working out, studying, and reading, I sometimes listen to music while taking a walk. However, I would say that I listen to it the most at the gym.

## MUSIC-HABIT-4 — 기기·앱과 음악의 의미

**장면:** 스마트폰·에어팟 → 멜론·유튜브 플레이리스트 → 기분과 집중 조절 → 없으면 상상하기 어려움

As for the device I use, I usually listen to music on my smartphone with my AirPods. They are convenient because I can carry them around and listen to music wherever I am.

I mostly use an app called Melon. I have created several playlists on it, so I can quickly choose one depending on my mood or what I am doing.

I also use playlists on YouTube, especially when I want to listen to a long classical music playlist while studying or reading.

What I like most about music is that it helps me concentrate in different ways. Rap music gives me energy and helps me focus on my workout, while classical music calms my mind and helps me focus on studying or reading.

So, for me, music is not just background noise. It is something that helps me control my mood and stay focused. Honestly, I cannot imagine working out or studying without it.

# 3. 음악 감상 취향의 변화

> 원본: [[01_설문스크립트/음악/음악 감상 취향의 변화|음악 취향 변화]]

## MUSIC-CHANGE-1 — 과거에는 한국 힙합 중심

**장면:** 취향이 많이 변함 → 초등학교부터 힙합 → 다이나믹 듀오·이센스·빈지노 → 비트와 가사

Come to think of it, my taste in music has changed quite a lot over the years.

When I was in elementary school, I mainly listened to Korean hip-hop on YouTube.

I was really into rappers like Dynamic Duo, E-Sens, and Beenzino. Back then, I especially liked songs with strong beats and interesting lyrics.

I enjoyed listening to the different ways rappers expressed their thoughts and told stories through their music.

## MUSIC-CHANGE-2 — 대학 공부 중 가사가 방해됨

**장면:** 대학 입학 후 변화 → 랩과 R&B를 들으며 시험공부 → 가사에 신경 → 같은 부분 반복

However, my taste in music started to change after I entered university.

One day, I was studying for an exam while listening to rap and R&B songs as usual.

However, I kept paying attention to the lyrics, especially when one of my favorite songs came on.

Because of that, I sometimes lost focus and had to read the same part over and over again.

## MUSIC-CHANGE-3 — 클래식을 발견함

**장면:** 가사 없는 음악을 시도 → 유튜브 클래식 플레이리스트 → 예상보다 집중 잘됨

Then I became curious and thought, “Why don't I try listening to music without any lyrics?”

So, I searched for a classical music playlist on YouTube and put it on while I studied.

Surprisingly, I was able to concentrate much better. The music was calm, and there were no lyrics to distract me.

## MUSIC-CHANGE-4 — 현재는 상황별로 선택

**장면:** 운동에는 랩 → 공부·독서에는 클래식 → 취향이 넓어짐 → 집중 도구가 됨

Since then, I have chosen music depending on what I am doing.

When I work out, I usually listen to rap because the strong beats give me energy and help me push myself.

On the other hand, when I study or read a book, I prefer classical music because it helps me clear my mind and stay focused.

So, I would say that my taste in music has become much broader than it used to be. In the past, I mostly listened to music for fun, but now I also use it to improve my concentration.

Honestly, I cannot imagine my daily life without music. Without it, working out and studying would be much more boring, and I think I would be less focused and less efficient.

# 4. 음악 감상 도중 기억에 남는 경험

> 원본: [[01_설문스크립트/음악/음악 감상 도중 겪은 기억에 남는 경험|음악 경험]]

## MUSIC-MEM-1 — 빈지노가 온 대학교 축제

**장면:** 작년 가을 축제 → 좋아하는 래퍼 방문 → 오랜 팬이라 설렘 → 친구들과 함께 가기로 함

One of the most memorable music experiences I've had happened at my university festival last fall.

Beenzino, my favorite Korean rapper, came to perform at the festival.

I've been listening to his music for years, so I was really excited when I heard that he was coming.

Some of my close friends also liked his music, so we decided to go to the concert together.

## MUSIC-MEM-2 — 공연 전과 빈지노 등장

**장면:** 좋은 자리를 위해 일찍 감 → 이미 많은 학생 → 에너지 넘침 → 관객 열광

On the day of the festival, we headed over to the outdoor stage a little early because we wanted to get a good spot.

There were already a lot of students waiting, and the whole place was full of energy.

When Beenzino finally came out on stage, the crowd went wild.

My friends and I shouted, sang along, and recorded a few short videos on our phones.

## MUSIC-MEM-3 — Aqua Man 라이브

**장면:** 가장 좋았던 곡 → 입문곡 → 평소 에어팟과 다른 라이브 → 모두 함께 따라 부름

The best part was when he performed **Aqua Man**.

It was the first song that really got me into his music, and I've listened to it many times over the years.

I usually listen to it through my AirPods, but hearing it live felt completely different.

The beat was much louder, and everyone around us started singing along. My friends and I knew the song well, so we sang along together and had a great time.

## MUSIC-MEM-4 — 친구들과 공유해서 더 특별했던 경험

**장면:** 좋아하는 곡을 친구들과 라이브로 들음 → 평소보다 특별 → 지금도 곡을 들으면 떠오름 → 다시 보고 싶음

What made the experience so memorable was that I got to hear one of my favorite songs live with my close friends.

I usually put on Beenzino's music while I'm working out, driving, or taking a walk, and it always puts me in a good mood.

However, that night felt even more special because I was able to share the experience with my friends.

Even now, whenever I listen to **Aqua Man**, I still remember that festival.

It was a simple experience, but I had an amazing time. That's why it remains one of my most memorable music experiences, and I would definitely love to see him perform live again someday.
