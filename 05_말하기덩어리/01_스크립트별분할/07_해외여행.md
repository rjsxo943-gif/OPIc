# 1. 가장 좋아하는 해외 여행지

> 원본: [[01_설문스크립트/해외여행/가장 좋아하는 해외 여행지|가장 좋아하는 해외 여행지]]

## TRAVEL-FAV-1 — 여러 나라 중 몽골 선택

**장면:** 여러 국가 방문 → 모두 달랐음 → 가장 좋아하는 곳은 몽골 → 친구 2명과 6박 7일

I've traveled to several places overseas, including Thailand, Vietnam, Laos, Cambodia, Japan, Hong Kong, and Mongolia.

They were all interesting in different ways, but if I had to choose my favorite destination, I would definitely go with Mongolia.

I went there during my summer vacation last year with two of my college friends, and we stayed there for six nights and seven days.

## TRAVEL-FAV-2 — 자연과 날씨

**장면:** 자연을 좋아함 → 여름인데 덥지 않음 → 초원·평원·고원·고비사막 → 거대한 풍경

The main reason I loved Mongolia so much is that I am a huge fan of nature.

The weather was also perfect for traveling. Even though it was summer, it was not too hot, so we could spend a lot of time outdoors without feeling exhausted.

What impressed me most was the variety of landscapes. There were endless green fields, wide-open plains, highlands, and the Gobi Desert.

Some parts of the Gobi looked like the Grand Canyon because of the huge cliffs and rock formations. Everything looked massive and untouched, so I felt very small compared to the scenery around me.

The night sky was also incredible. Since there were hardly any city lights, I could clearly see countless stars and even different constellations. I had never seen the sky that clearly before.

## TRAVEL-FAV-3 — 비 내린 사막과 꽃을 먹은 경험

**장면:** 비가 드문 곳에 폭우 → 사막에 물과 꽃 → 가이드 권유 → 파 맛이 난 꽃

Another special thing was the weather.

Our guide told us that it usually did not rain much in that area, but it rained heavily while we were there.

Because of the rain, we were able to see water flowing through parts of the desert and small flowers blooming from the ground.

Our guide even told us that we could try eating one of the flowers. I was curious, so I tasted it, and surprisingly, it tasted a little like green onion.

It was such a strange and unique experience that I still remember it clearly.

## TRAVEL-FAV-4 — 음식과 추천

**장면:** 음식과 보드카도 잘 맞음 → 다른 나라와 완전히 다름 → 자연을 좋아하는 사람에게 추천

On top of that, Mongolian food suited my taste better than I expected. I enjoyed the meat dishes, dumplings, and local vodka as well.

Overall, Mongolia was completely different from any other country I had visited.

It had beautiful scenery, unusual experiences, delicious food, and a peaceful atmosphere.

I would strongly recommend Mongolia to anyone who enjoys nature. It is the kind of place that makes you forget about city life and simply appreciate the world around you.

# 2. 가장 기억에 남는 여행 경험

> 원본: [[01_설문스크립트/해외여행/가장 기억에 남는 여행 경험|가장 기억에 남는 여행 경험]]

## TRAVEL-MEM-1 — 몽골 여행과 게르의 밤

**장면:** 지난여름 몽골 → 대학 친구 2명 → 6박 7일 → 전통 게르 → 별을 보러 밖으로 나감

One of the most memorable travel experiences I have ever had happened in Mongolia last summer.

I went there during my summer vacation with two of my college friends, so there were three of us in total.

We traveled around Mongolia for six nights and seven days, and one night, we stayed in a traditional Mongolian ger.

After dinner, we went back to our ger and rested for a while. Later that night, we decided to go outside because we wanted to see the stars.

## TRAVEL-MEM-2 — 간식과 보드카를 들고 별을 봄

**장면:** 건망고·과자·보드카 → 의자에는 음식 → 우리는 땅에 앉음 → 셀 수 없는 별

We brought out some dried mangoes, snacks, and a bottle of Mongolian vodka.

We placed everything on a chair, but instead of sitting on chairs ourselves, we just sat on the ground together.

There were almost no lights around the ger camp, so the sky was incredibly clear.

I could see countless stars and even several constellations. I had seen stars before, of course, but I had never seen that many at once. The sky felt much bigger than it did back home.

## TRAVEL-MEM-3 — 친구들과 대화하고 개들과 어울림

**장면:** 만두·보드카·연애 이야기 → 농담 → 큰 개들이 처음엔 무서움 → 실제로 친근하고 곁을 지킴

While looking at the stars, we drank some vodka and talked about all kinds of things.

We talked about the dumplings we had eaten at a local restaurant earlier that day, and we discussed whether we preferred Chinggis Khan Gold or Eden vodka.

We also talked about relationships and joked around like we normally did.

There were several large dogs living near the ger camp as well. At first, they looked a little intimidating because they were so big.

However, they were actually very friendly. They stayed close to us, played with us, and seemed to be watching over the area.

## TRAVEL-MEM-4 — 평범한 밤이 가장 특별했던 이유

**장면:** 학교·일 걱정 없음 → 여행에만 집중 → 유명 관광지가 아님 → 사람과 분위기 때문에 완벽함

What made that night so special was that we were completely focused on the trip.

We were not thinking about school, work, or anything waiting for us back home. We were simply sitting under the stars, having drinks, eating snacks, and enjoying each other's company.

It was not an expensive activity or a famous tourist attraction. In fact, it was a very simple night.

However, the peaceful grassland, the clear sky, my close friends, and even the dogs made it feel almost perfect.

Even now, when I think about Mongolia, that is the first night that comes to mind. It reminded me that the most memorable moments during a trip are not always the ones we plan in advance.

Sometimes, an ordinary moment becomes unforgettable because of the people you are with and the atmosphere around you.

# 3. 여행 전 준비하는 활동 경향

> 원본: [[01_설문스크립트/해외여행/여행 전 준비하는 활동 경향|여행 준비 경향]]

## TRAVEL-PREP-1 — 빡빡하게 계획하지 않는 여행 스타일

**장면:** 해외여행을 좋아함 → 모든 세부 계획은 안 함 → 동행인이 항공권·숙소 → 함께 큰 계획만 논의

I really enjoy traveling abroad because I can experience different cultures, food, and ways of life.

However, I am not the kind of person who plans every single detail before a trip.

I usually travel with friends or someone close to me, and they often take care of booking the flights and hotels.

We normally discuss the main plan together, but honestly, I am quite flexible because I can have a good time almost anywhere.

## TRAVEL-PREP-2 — 맛집 중심의 느슨한 일정

**장면:** 유튜브로 정보 확인 → 음식은 직접 조사 → 하루 2~3곳 → 빡빡한 일정 싫어함

Before traveling, I sometimes watch YouTube videos about the destination.

The people I travel with usually do more research than I do, but there is one thing I always care about, which is food.

I love eating, so I usually look up popular local restaurants and dishes that I should try. Transportation is not a major concern for me because I can use Google Maps or take a taxi if necessary.

I also do not like making a packed schedule. I normally choose only two or three restaurants, landmarks, or activities for each day. Sometimes, I do not make a detailed plan at all.

## TRAVEL-PREP-3 — 현지 일상과 가벼운 짐

**장면:** 시장·랜드마크·우연히 찾은 식당 → 관광지 사이를 서두르지 않음 → 여권·충전기·약·달러

I prefer walking around local markets, visiting famous landmarks, and checking out restaurants I happen to find.

I think this helps me experience the everyday life of the country instead of simply rushing from one tourist attraction to another.

When it comes to packing, I try to pack light.

I mainly bring my passport, a charger, a portable battery, some medicine, and US dollars.

I exchange money in advance, but I do not bring too many clothes or other items because I can buy whatever I need after I arrive.

## TRAVEL-PREP-4 — 공항 밖으로 나가는 순간의 설렘

**장면:** 예약이나 짐 싸기보다 → 입국 심사 후 공항 밖 → 새로운 나라 첫 장면 → 여행이 시작됨

For me, the most exciting moment is not booking the flight or packing my suitcase.

It is when I finish going through immigration, walk out of the airport, and see the new country for the first time.

At that moment, I finally feel that my trip has really begun, and I always get excited about everything that is waiting for me.
