# 공통 말하기 요소

> 여러 스크립트에서 반복해서 사용할 수 있는 10~20초짜리 말하기 블록이다. 대괄호 안의 내용만 질문에 맞게 바꾼다.

---

# 1. 도입 요소

## INTRO-FAV-01 — 가장 좋아하는 대상

**키워드:** 여러 대상 → 하나 선택 → 가장 좋아함

I've been to or experienced quite a few **[places or things]**, but if I had to choose just one, it would definitely be **[name]**. What I like most about it is **[main reason]**.

## INTRO-HABIT-01 — 평소 활동 경향

**키워드:** 일상의 일부 → 자주 함 → 상황에 따라 달라짐

**[Activity]** has become a big part of my daily routine. I do it quite regularly, although the way I do it usually depends on my mood, schedule, or what I need at the time.

## INTRO-EXP-01 — 기억에 남는 경험

**키워드:** 기억에 남음 → 언제·어디서 → 아직 선명함

One of the most memorable experiences I've had happened **[time and place]**. I was with **[person or people]**, and I still remember it clearly because something special or unexpected happened that day.

## INTRO-COMP-01 — 과거와 현재 비교

**키워드:** 많이 달라짐 → 과거 → 현재 비교

Come to think of it, **[topic]** has changed quite a lot over the years. The way it was in the past was very different from the way it is now, so let me compare the two.

## INTRO-CHANGE-01 — 내가 만든 변화

**키워드:** 큰 변화는 아님 → 작은 변화 → 더 좋아짐

I haven't made any huge changes to **[place or habit]**, but I have made a few small changes to make it more comfortable or useful. Those changes may sound simple, but they made a noticeable difference.

## INTRO-ISSUE-01 — 이슈와 의견

**키워드:** 사람마다 다름 → 여러 이유 → 하나의 정답 없음

To be honest, I think people have different opinions about **[issue]** because their situations and goals are not the same. There isn't one perfect answer, so it makes sense to look at it from more than one side.

# 2. 장소와 분위기 요소

## PLACE-ACCESS-01 — 위치와 접근성

**키워드:** 가까움 → 이동 시간 → 자주 가기 편함

It's located quite close to where I live. It usually takes me about **[time]** to get there **[on foot/by bus/by car]**, so it's convenient and easy for me to visit without making a complicated plan.

## PLACE-ATM-01 — 활기와 평화로움

**키워드:** 사람 많음 → 너무 붐비지는 않음 → 편안함

The place is usually lively because there are quite a few people walking around and enjoying themselves. Still, it normally isn't too crowded, so the atmosphere feels comfortable rather than noisy or stressful.

## PLACE-NATURE-01 — 자연과 오감 묘사

**키워드:** 풍경 → 바람·소리·향기 → 도시를 잊음

What I like most is the natural scenery. I can feel the fresh air, hear **[the waves/birds/the wind]**, and enjoy the view, so for a while, it feels like I'm far away from the middle of the city.

## PLACE-DAYNIGHT-01 — 낮과 밤 분위기 비교

**키워드:** 낮에는 활기 → 저녁에는 평화 → 둘 다 좋음

During the daytime, the area feels lively and energetic. Later in the evening, it becomes much calmer and more peaceful, so I enjoy the place in two completely different ways.

## PLACE-SMALLCOZY-01 — 작지만 편안한 공간

**키워드:** 크지 않음 → 필요한 것만 있음 → 아늑함

It isn't a large space, and I don't keep a lot of things there. Still, it has everything I really need, which makes it feel simple, uncluttered, and surprisingly cozy.

# 3. 사람 요소

## PEOPLE-ALONE-01 — 혼자 보내는 시간

**키워드:** 혼자 감 → 내 속도 → 방해받지 않음

I usually do it alone because I like moving at my own pace. I can choose exactly what I want to do without waiting for anyone or worrying about disturbing other people.

## PEOPLE-FRIENDS-01 — 친구와 함께 출발

**키워드:** 친구와 함께 → 피곤하거나 스트레스 → 같이 가기로 함

I went there with a few of my close friends. We had all been busy or stressed, so we decided to take a break, spend some time together, and get away from our usual routine.

## PEOPLE-FAMILY-01 — 가족과 보내는 시간

**키워드:** 가족이 모임 → 음식 → 평범한 대화

When my family is around, we usually order or prepare something simple to eat together. We talk about ordinary things and enjoy being in the same place, which makes the time feel warm and comfortable.

## PEOPLE-SHARE-01 — 경험을 함께한 의미

**키워드:** 혼자보다 특별함 → 함께 반응 → 추억 공유

The experience felt more special because I was able to share it with people close to me. We reacted together, talked about it afterwards, and turned a simple moment into a memory we could all remember.

# 4. 활동 요소

## ACT-WALK-01 — 걷기와 풍경 감상

**키워드:** 천천히 걷기 → 사진 → 주변 감상

We took a long walk around the area and looked at the scenery. We also took a few pictures, but most of the time, we simply walked slowly and enjoyed what was around us.

## ACT-SIT-TALK-01 — 앉아서 먹고 이야기하기

**키워드:** 자리 잡기 → 간식·음료 → 오래 대화

We found a comfortable spot, sat down, and brought out some snacks and drinks. We talked about our lives for a while and enjoyed the atmosphere without rushing to do anything else.

## ACT-REST-01 — 집에서 쉬는 활동

**키워드:** 눕기 → 유튜브·독서 → 아무것도 안 함

After a long day, I usually lie down and watch YouTube or read something. Sometimes, I don't do anything at all and just give myself time to relax and recharge.

## ACT-ROUTINE-01 — 활동을 순서대로 설명

**키워드:** 먼저 → 그다음 → 마무리

First, I **[first activity]**. After that, I usually **[second activity]**, and if I still have enough time or energy, I **[optional activity]** before finishing.

## ACT-FOOD-01 — 음식 중심 활동

**키워드:** 맛집·현지 음식 → 직접 먹어봄 → 기억에 남음

Food is one of the things I care about most. I like looking up local dishes or restaurants, trying something new, and remembering a place through the food I had there.

## ACT-PACKLIGHT-01 — 필요한 것만 준비

**키워드:** 짐 가볍게 → 필수품 → 나머지는 현지 구매

I try to pack light and bring only the essentials, such as **[items]**. I don't take too many extra things because I can usually buy whatever I need after I arrive.

# 5. 경험 전개 요소

## EXP-UNEXPECTED-01 — 예상 밖 상황

**키워드:** 예상 못 함 → 갑자기 발견 → 놀람

While I was **[activity]**, I suddenly noticed something I hadn't expected at all. For a moment, I didn't know how to react because the situation was completely different from what I had imagined.

## EXP-EMOTION-01 — 당황과 불편함

**키워드:** 충격 → 당황 → 어색함

I was completely shocked, and the situation quickly became awkward. To make things worse, I wasn't prepared for it at all, so I felt embarrassed and wanted to leave as soon as possible.

## EXP-PROBLEM-01 — 문제 발견

**키워드:** 문제 발견 → 원인 모름 → 확인

At first, I noticed that something was wrong, but I didn't know what was causing the problem. I checked the area carefully and eventually found where it was coming from.

## EXP-SOLUTION-01 — 도움을 요청해 해결

**키워드:** 혼자 해결 못 함 → 연락 → 기다림 → 해결

I couldn't solve it by myself, so I contacted someone who knew how to handle it and explained the situation. I had to wait for a while, but the problem was finally fixed.

## EXP-LOOKBACK-01 — 당시와 지금의 감정 변화

**키워드:** 당시 불편 → 지금은 웃김 → 추억이 됨

At the time, the situation felt very uncomfortable. However, when I look back on it now, I can laugh about it, and that is probably why it has become such an unforgettable memory.

## EXP-SIMPLE-MEANING-01 — 평범하지만 의미 있었던 순간

**키워드:** 비싸거나 특별하지 않음 → 사람·분위기 → 기억에 남음

It wasn't an expensive activity or a famous tourist attraction. Still, the people I was with and the atmosphere around us made the ordinary moment feel surprisingly special.

**키워드:** 둘 다 좋아함 → 지금은 한쪽 필요 → 이유

In my opinion, there is no single perfect choice. The most important thing is to understand what you need and choose the option that matches that purpose.
