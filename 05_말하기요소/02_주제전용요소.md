# 집

## HOME-01 — 스튜디오 아파트 소개

**키워드:** 혼자 거주 → 3년 → 한 공간

I live alone in a small studio apartment, and I've been living there for about three years. Since everything is in one open space, I technically don't have separate rooms.

## HOME-02 — 침대 주변 배치

**키워드:** 창문 옆 침대 → 작은 책상 → 베이지 러그

My favorite part of my home is the corner around my bed. My bed is right next to the window, and I also have a small desk and a thick beige rug on the floor.

## HOME-03 — 침대 주변에서 하는 일

**키워드:** 유튜브 → 독서 → 쉬기

After a long day, I usually lie down on my bed and watch YouTube. Sometimes, I read a book at my desk, or I simply rest without doing anything.

## HOME-04 — 과거 가족 집

**키워드:** 가족과 거주 → 넓은 아파트 → 함께 보낸 공간

In the past, I lived with my family in a spacious apartment. It had several bedrooms, two bathrooms, and a large living room where my family often spent time together.

## HOME-05 — 침대·러그·가구 변화

**키워드:** 새 침대 → 베이지 러그 → 창문 쪽 재배치

I bought a new bed because my old one was uncomfortable, and I also added a thick beige rug. Later, I moved my bed and desk closer to the window, which made the space warmer, brighter, and much cozier.

## HOME-06 — 욕실 누수

**키워드:** 토요일 아침 → 바닥의 물 → 변기 누수

One Saturday morning, I woke up and found the bathroom floor covered with water. After checking the area carefully, I noticed that the water was leaking from the toilet.

# 공원

## PARK-01 — 북항친수공원 위치

**키워드:** 부산역 뒤 → 도보 15분 → 자주 방문

The park I visit is called North Port Waterfront Park, and it is right behind Busan Station. It takes me about fifteen minutes to get there on foot, so I can visit it regularly.

## PARK-02 — 시설과 봄 정원

**키워드:** 산책로·벤치 → 운동 시설 → 꽃과 향기

The park has walking trails, plenty of benches, soccer fields, basketball courts, and beautiful gardens. The gardens are my favorite part because the flowers blossom in spring and give off an incredible scent.

## PARK-03 — 새벽 러닝 루틴

**키워드:** 새벽 5시 30분 → 3km → 운동기구·스트레칭

I usually go there alone at the crack of dawn and run about three kilometers. If I still have enough energy, I use the outdoor workout machines, stretch my arms and legs, and cool down.

## PARK-04 — 전 여자친구를 본 사건

**키워드:** 예전 벤치 → 다른 남자 → 모른 척 떠남

I walked toward a bench where I used to sit with my ex-girlfriend. I suddenly saw her sitting there with another guy, so I pretended not to see them and quickly walked away.

# 해변과 캠핑

## BEACH-01 — 광안리 위치와 접근

**키워드:** 수영구 → 버스 30분 → 친구와 방문

My favorite beach is Gwangalli Beach in Suyeong-gu, Busan. It takes about thirty minutes to get there by bus, so I normally visit it with friends when the weather is nice.

## BEACH-02 — 돗자리와 산책

**키워드:** 돗자리 → 간식과 대화 → 물가 산책

We usually spread out a picnic mat on the sand, eat snacks, and talk for a while. Later, we take a long walk near the water and take a few pictures.

## BEACH-03 — 광안대교 야경

**키워드:** 일몰 → 어두워짐 → 다리 조명

The sunset is beautiful, but the night view is even more impressive. As it gets darker, Gwangandaegyo Bridge lights up and shines over the water.

## CAMP-01 — 캠핑 장비

**키워드:** 텐트·침낭 → 의자·테이블 → 버너·그릴

The most important items are a tent, sleeping bags, camping chairs, and a small table. For cooking, we also bring a portable stove, a small grill, and some basic cooking tools.

## CAMP-02 — 개인 물품과 음식

**키워드:** 옷·수건 → 고기·채소·라면 → 선크림·충전기

We pack a change of clothes, towels, meat, vegetables, instant noodles, snacks, and drinks. We also make sure to bring water, sunscreen, insect repellent, a portable charger, and a Bluetooth speaker.

## CAMP-03 — 송정 캠핑 시작

**키워드:** 여름 → 친구 3명 → 송정 → 텐트 협력

Earlier this summer, I went on a two-day, one-night camping trip near Songjeong Beach with three close friends. Pitching the tent was harder than expected, so all four of us had to work together.

## CAMP-04 — 캠핑 저녁과 밤

**키워드:** 고기와 채소 → 일몰 → 음악과 대화

In the evening, we cooked meat and vegetables on a small grill while watching the sunset. Later, we sat outside the tent, listened to music, and talked about our lives for hours.

# 음악

## MUSIC-01 — 빈지노 소개와 스타일

**키워드:** 한국 래퍼 → 독특한 목소리 → 재즈 힙합

Beenzino is my favorite Korean rapper, and I've been listening to his music for years. I especially like his unique voice, polished rap style, and the way he mixes jazz hip-hop with modern hip-hop.

## MUSIC-02 — 24:26과 Aqua Man

**키워드:** 좋아하는 앨범 → 청춘과 일상 → 입문곡

My favorite album is **24:26** because its songs talk about youth and everyday life. The first song that really got me into his music was **Aqua Man**, and I've listened to it countless times.

## MUSIC-03 — 운동에는 랩, 공부에는 클래식

**키워드:** 강한 비트 → 운동 집중 → 가사 없는 클래식

When I work out, I listen to energetic Korean hip-hop because the strong beats give me energy. When I study or read, I prefer classical music without lyrics because it helps me clear my mind and concentrate.

## MUSIC-04 — 에어팟·멜론·유튜브

**키워드:** 스마트폰·에어팟 → 멜론 → 긴 클래식 재생목록

I usually listen to music on my smartphone with my AirPods. I mostly use Melon, but I also use long YouTube playlists when I want classical music in the background.

## MUSIC-05 — 대학교 축제 공연

**키워드:** 작년 가을 → 야외무대 → 관객 열광

Beenzino performed at my university festival last fall, so my friends and I went to the outdoor stage early to get a good spot. When he came out, the whole place was full of energy and the crowd went wild.

## MUSIC-06 — Aqua Man 라이브

**키워드:** 평소 이어폰 → 라이브 차이 → 함께 따라 부름

The best part was hearing **Aqua Man** live. I normally listen to it through my AirPods, but the live performance felt completely different, and my friends and I sang along together.

# 해외여행

## TRAVEL-01 — 몽골 여행 기본 정보

**키워드:** 작년 여름 → 대학 친구 2명 → 6박 7일

I traveled around Mongolia for six nights and seven days during my summer vacation last year. I went with two of my college friends, so there were three of us in total.

## TRAVEL-02 — 몽골 자연환경

**키워드:** 초원·평원 → 고비사막 → 거대한 암석

Mongolia had endless green fields, wide-open plains, highlands, and the Gobi Desert. Some rock formations looked like the Grand Canyon, and the huge untouched landscape made me feel very small.

## TRAVEL-03 — 사막의 비와 꽃

**키워드:** 폭우 → 물이 흐름 → 꽃 → 파 맛

It rained heavily in an area where rain was usually rare, so we saw water flowing through the desert and small flowers blooming. Our guide told me I could taste one, and surprisingly, it tasted a little like green onion.

## TRAVEL-04 — 게르 밖 별밤

**키워드:** 게르 → 건망고·보드카 → 땅에 앉음 → 별

One night, we stayed in a traditional Mongolian ger and went outside to see the stars. We brought dried mangoes, snacks, and vodka, placed them on a chair, and sat together on the ground under the clear sky.

## TRAVEL-05 — 친구들과 대화

**키워드:** 만두·보드카 → 연애 이야기 → 농담

While looking at the stars, we drank vodka and talked about local food, the different vodkas we had tried, and our relationships. We joked around and enjoyed each other's company without thinking about school or work.

## TRAVEL-06 — 게르의 큰 개들

**키워드:** 처음에는 무서움 → 실제로 친근함 → 곁을 지킴

Several large dogs stayed near the ger camp. They looked intimidating at first, but they were friendly, played with us, and seemed to watch over the area.

## TRAVEL-07 — 유연한 여행 계획

**키워드:** 동행인이 예약 → 맛집 조사 → 하루 2~3곳

I don't plan every single detail before traveling. My companions often handle the flights and hotels, while I look up local food, and we usually choose only two or three main places or activities for each day.

## TRAVEL-08 — 현지 일상과 가벼운 짐

**키워드:** 시장·랜드마크 → 짐 가볍게 → 필수품

I prefer walking around local markets, visiting landmarks, and checking out restaurants I happen to find. I pack light and mainly bring my passport, charger, portable battery, medicine, and some cash.

## TRAVEL-09 — 공항 밖의 설렘

**키워드:** 입국 심사 → 공항 밖 → 여행 시작

The most exciting moment is when I finish immigration and walk out of the airport. That is when I finally feel that the trip has really begun.

# 집에서 보내는 휴가

## VAC-01 — 집에서 보내는 아침

**키워드:** 오전 10시 → 물·세안·아침 → 느긋함

On a typical day off, I wake up at around ten, drink a large glass of water, wash my face, and have a simple breakfast. I don't rush because I want the day to feel completely different from a regular weekday.

## VAC-02 — 유튜브·웹툰·독서

**키워드:** 여행 유튜브 → 웹툰 → 책 → 생산성 강요 안 함

I usually lie down and watch travel videos or read webtoons. If I have an interesting book, I read for a while, but I don't force myself to exercise or be productive.

## VAC-03 — 시험이 끝난 뒤의 휴가

**키워드:** 기말 종료 → 본가 → 아무 일정 없음

My most memorable stay-at-home vacation was the Monday after my final tests ended. I returned to my parents' home with no classes, deadlines, or plans, so I felt completely free for the first time in a long while.

## VAC-04 — 가족과 치킨·피자

**키워드:** 저녁 → 가족 퇴근 → 음식과 대화

Later in the evening, my family came home from work. We ordered chicken and pizza, talked about ordinary things, and simply enjoyed being together.

## VAC-05 — 휴가의 두 목적

**키워드:** 새로운 경험 → 조용한 회복 → 목적에 따라 다름

Some people use vacations to travel, try new food, and create memories. Others need to sleep, stay home, and recover, so the best kind of vacation depends on what the person needs.

I'm currently a senior majoring in electronic engineering, and I'm expected to graduate soon. These days, I'm preparing for an engineering career and becoming especially interested in smart factories and automation systems.
