# 공원 — 기억에 남는 경험 75초 버전

## 질문

- Tell me about a memorable experience you had at a park.
- What is the most memorable thing that happened to you at a park?

## Script

One of my most memorable experiences at the park happened last fall.

There was a small weekend festival, so I went there alone to relax. There were food trucks, small booths, and live music, so the atmosphere was really lively.

While I was walking around, I decided to pass by a bench that I used to sit on with my ex-girlfriend. We had visited the park together many times when we were dating.

But when I got close to the bench, I suddenly saw her sitting there with another guy. I really didn't expect that, so I was completely shocked.

To make things worse, I was wearing very casual clothes, so I felt even more awkward. I pretended not to see them and quickly walked away.

At the time, it was really uncomfortable, but now I think it's kind of funny. That's why I still remember that day so clearly.

## 뜻

공원에서 겪은 가장 기억에 남는 경험 중 하나는 지난가을에 있었어.

주말에 작은 축제가 열려서 혼자 쉬러 갔어. 푸드트럭과 작은 부스, 라이브 음악이 있어서 분위기가 정말 활기찼어.

공원을 걷다가 예전에 전 여자친구와 자주 앉았던 벤치 쪽을 지나가 보기로 했어. 사귈 때 그 공원에 함께 자주 갔었거든.

그런데 벤치에 가까이 갔을 때 갑자기 전 여자친구가 다른 남자와 앉아 있는 걸 봤어. 전혀 예상하지 못해서 정말 충격을 받았어.

게다가 나는 아주 편한 옷을 입고 있어서 더 민망하고 어색했어. 결국 못 본 척하고 빠르게 그 자리를 떠났어.

당시에는 정말 불편했지만 지금 생각하면 조금 웃긴 일이야. 그래서 아직도 그날이 아주 선명하게 기억나.
