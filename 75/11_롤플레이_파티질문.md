# 롤플레이 — 초대받은 파티에 관해 질문하기

## Script
Hi, Minsoo. It's GeonTae. I'm calling because I have a few questions about the party this weekend.

First, where is the party going to be? Is it at your place? Oh, it is? Could you send me your address? I've never been there before.

Also, do I need to bring anything? Oh, you're preparing most of the food? That's great. Then I'll bring some drinks and snacks.

One more thing. What time does the party start? Seven o'clock? That works for me.

By the way, who else is coming? Are they mostly people I already know? Oh, I see. It'll be nice to meet some new people too.

Thanks for letting me know. I'll see you at your place at seven on Saturday. I'm really looking forward to it. Bye!
