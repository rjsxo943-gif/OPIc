# 호텔 - 기억에 남는 호텔 경험

## Question

- Tell me about a memorable experience you had at a hotel.
- Where was the hotel?
- Who were you with?
- What made the experience memorable?

---

## Speaking Order

친구와 홍콩 여행

↓

작지만 깨끗했던 호텔

↓

침사추이와 빅토리아 하버 방문

↓

홍콩의 야경과 도시 분위기

↓

다시 가고 싶은 여행

---

## Full Script

One of my most memorable hotel experiences happened during a trip to Hong Kong with a close friend.

Our trip was quite short, so we wanted to stay somewhere convenient. We chose a hotel near public transportation and some of the major tourist areas.

When we first entered the room, I was surprised by how small it was. Hong Kong was more expensive than Korea, so the room was smaller than what I had expected for the price.

Still, it was clean, the bed was comfortable, and the location was excellent. We could easily get to Tsim Sha Tsui and Victoria Harbour.

During the day, we walked around the busy streets, visited shops, and tried different local foods. At night, we went to Victoria Harbour and looked at the lights from the buildings across the water.

The skyline was amazing, and the whole city felt energetic and beautiful. It was completely different from the natural scenery I had seen in Mongolia.

After walking around for hours, we headed back to the hotel, bought some snacks, and talked about everything we had seen that day.

The hotel itself was not luxurious, but its convenient location made our short trip much easier. It became part of my memory of Hong Kong’s bright lights, busy streets, and unique culture.

Even though the trip was short and quite expensive, I really enjoyed it. I would love to visit Hong Kong again and stay in the same area.

---

## Key Expressions

- somewhere convenient
- smaller than I had expected
- close to the tourist areas
- head back to the hotel
- become part of my memory
