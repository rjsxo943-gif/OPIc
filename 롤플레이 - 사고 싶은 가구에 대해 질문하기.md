# 롤플레이 - 사고 싶은 가구에 대해 질문하기

## Situation

새 아파트로 이사해서 침대, 소파, 테이블 같은 가구를 사려고 가구 매장을 방문한 상황이다.

판매 직원에게 필요한 가구의 종류, 크기, 가격, 할인 등에 관해 세 가지 또는 네 가지 질문을 한다.

---

## Speaking Order

인사와 상황 설명

↓

싱글 침대의 종류와 가격 질문

↓

편안한 소파와 테이블 세트 질문

↓

배송과 조립 서비스 질문

↓

할인 행사 질문

↓

감사 인사 후 마무리

---

## Full Script

Hi, I just moved into a new apartment, so I need to buy a few pieces of furniture.

My place is a small studio, and it is almost empty right now. I am looking for a bed, a sofa, and maybe a small table.

Could I ask you a few questions?

First of all, what options do you have for a single-size bed?

I live alone, and my apartment is not very large, so I do not need a queen-size bed.

Oh, I see. That model looks nice.

How much is it, and does the price include the mattress?

Okay, that is good to know.

Also, do you have any comfortable sofas that would fit in a small living room?

I spend a lot of time relaxing on the couch, so comfort is very important to me.

Do you have a matching coffee table as well?

Oh, that set sounds perfect.

One more thing. Do you offer delivery and assembly services?

My apartment is upstairs, so it would be difficult for me to carry and assemble everything by myself.

Great. That would make things much easier.

Finally, are there any special discounts or promotions this month?

Do you offer a discount if I buy the bed, sofa, and table together?

A ten-percent discount? That sounds great.

Thank you for answering all my questions. You have been very helpful.

I will look around the store for a little longer and let you know when I make my decision.

Thanks again.

---

## 핵심 질문

### 침대 종류

What options do you have for a single-size bed?

Do you have a bed that would fit in a small studio?

### 가격과 구성품

How much is it?

Does the price include the mattress?

### 소파와 테이블

Do you have any comfortable sofas for a small living room?

Do you have a matching coffee table?

### 배송과 조립

Do you offer delivery and assembly services?

How long does delivery usually take?

### 할인

Are there any special discounts or promotions this month?

Do you offer a discount if I buy several items together?

---

## 공통 반응 표현

- Oh, I see.
- That looks nice.
- That is good to know.
- That sounds perfect.
- That would make things much easier.
- That sounds great.

---

## 재사용 가능한 가구 질문

- What colors does it come in?
- Do you have this in a smaller size?
- Is there a warranty?
- Can I return or exchange it?
- Is the delivery fee included?
- When is the earliest delivery date?
- Can I see another model?
- Is this item currently in stock?

## Notes

- 핵심 순서는 `침대 → 소파·테이블 → 배송·조립 → 할인`이다.
- 질문을 잊어버리면 `가격, 크기, 배송` 세 가지만 물어도 대응할 수 있다.
- `a few pieces of furniture`에서 `furniture`는 셀 수 없는 명사이므로 `furnitures`라고 하지 않는다.
- `I live alone in a small studio`는 집 관련 스크립트에서도 재사용할 수 있다.
