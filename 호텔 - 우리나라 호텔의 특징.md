# 호텔 - 우리나라 호텔의 특징

## Question

- Tell me about hotels in your country.
- What kinds of hotels are there?
- What are they usually like?

---

## Speaking Order

한국에는 다양한 호텔이 있음

↓

도시의 비즈니스호텔과 고급호텔

↓

펜션·게스트하우스·한옥 숙소

↓

---

## Full Script

There are many different kinds of hotels in Korea, so travelers can choose one depending on their budget and the purpose of their trip.

In large cities such as Seoul and Busan, there are many business hotels near subway stations, train stations, and shopping areas. The rooms are usually not very large, but they are clean and have everything travelers need, such as a comfortable bed, a private bathroom, free Wi-Fi, and basic toiletries.

There are also luxury hotels with swimming pools, fitness centers, restaurants, and great city views. They are much more expensive, so people usually stay there for special occasions or relaxing vacations.

Outside major cities, travelers can also find pensions, guesthouses, and traditional Korean-style accommodations. Some tourists enjoy staying in a hanok because they can experience traditional Korean architecture and culture.

One thing I like about hotels in Korea is their convenient location. There are usually restaurants, cafés, convenience stores, and public transportation nearby.

Overall, Korean hotels are generally clean, safe, and convenient. Travelers can easily find a place that matches their travel style and budget.
